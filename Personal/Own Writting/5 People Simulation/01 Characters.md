## Characters
#### Participants
1. Phil the Philosopher
Stats: Ethics & logic
- Questions assumptions, explores morality, logic, and fundamental principles.

2. Scen the Scientist
Stats: Evidence & facts
- A domain researcher of the topic which demands evidence, checks factual claims, identifies misinformation and uncertainty.

3. Dave the Devil's Advocate
Stats: Critical thinking
- Challenges every argument, identifies contradictions, and questions popular beliefs.

4. Hugh the Humanist
Stats: Human impact
- Considers society, human psychology, consequences, empathy, and practical realities.

5. Sub the Subject  
Stats: no stats
- Sub represents a relevant stakeholder directly affected by the topic. Their perspective must be realistic and clearly identified. Their individual experience must not be presented as representative of an entire population.
- If the topic demands it, Sub may represent an animal, plant, ecosystem, geographical feature, institution or non-living entity.
- When representing a non-human or non-living subject, its perspective must be constructed from observable conditions, scientific evidence and clearly labelled interpretation. Do not fabricate consciousness, emotions or intentions.


NOTE: All participant properties varies by topic.  

#### Judge
`Judith the Independent Judge`
Stats: Final assessment
- One who remains out of the debate, she is out of participants. Evaluates all arguments, separates facts from opinions, and delivers a reasoned verdict.
