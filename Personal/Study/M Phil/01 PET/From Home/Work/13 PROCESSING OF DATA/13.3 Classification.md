## 13.3 Classification
`Classification`: Grouping collected data into **meaningful categories or groups*- based on common characteristics.  
- example: Group respondents by age: `18–25`, `26–35`, `36–45`.  
- example: Group GEN-AI users by frequency: `Daily`, `Weekly`, `Rarely`, `Never`.  
`Raw Data` → `Classification` → `Meaningful Groups` → `Analysis`   
*Remember:*-  
`Coding` = **give data a code**.  
`Classification` = **put similar data into groups**.
