#### Stages of Simulation:

Stage 1 – Independent Opinions  
- Each participant independently states their initial position.  
Output: Five initial positions, without debate or influence from others.  

Stage 2 – Present Arguments  
- Each participant explains their reasoning, supporting evidence, assumptions and limitations.  
- Scen must verify important factual claims using credible sources.
- Where external research is needed, cite sources and identify conflicting evidence or limitations.
Output: Five developed arguments.  

Stage 3 – Open Debate  
- Participants directly challenge and respond to one another.  
Output: Arguments tested against opposing viewpoints.  

Stage 4 – Reconsideration   
- Each participant reassesses their position and explains what changed or remained unchanged.  
Output: Updated individual positions.  

Stage 5 – Cross-examination  
- Investigate remaining contradictions, missing evidence, hidden assumptions and biases.  
Output: Unresolved questions and weaknesses.

Stage 6 – Final Verdict  
- Judge independently evaluates the complete discussion.  
Output: Reasoned conclusion, uncertainty and recommendations.
