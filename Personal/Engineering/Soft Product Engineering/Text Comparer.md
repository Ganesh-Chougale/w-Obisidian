2. Functionality
it is like any other text document comparer. it will find difference between left & right text area & high-light it
example:
I love mango juice <=> I love orange juice
"m" became "or"
"ang" remained same
"o" became "e"
- Left Side
Meaning: "Original"
Removed text → Red background
Changed text → Orange background
- Right Side
Meaning: "Modified"
Added text → Green background
Changed text → Orange background
