## 24.4 Oral Presentation

`Oral Presentation`: communicating **research findings verbally** to an audience in a clear and organised way.

A good oral presentation usually includes:
- `Introduction` → briefly explain the research problem and objectives.
- `Main Findings` → present the important results.
- `Explanation` → explain what the findings mean.
- `Conclusion` → state the main takeaway.
- `Questions` → answer questions from the audience.

- example: A researcher presents their study on `AI-assisted learning` at a seminar, explaining the problem, methods, findings, and conclusions using slides.

`Research` → `Prepare` → `Present Findings` → `Explain` → `Answer Questions`
