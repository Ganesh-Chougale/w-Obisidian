Part 1: Setup – Jibreel Se Pehli Guftagu
```
Makkah ke ek chhote se ghar mein, chandni raat ka andhera faila hua tha.

Aaminah bint Wahb, woh gori-chitti, moti gaand wali, badi-badi choochiyon wali aurat apne bistar par leti hui thi. Uski choochiyan kapde ke andar bhi ubhari hui thi, aur uski moti, ras bhari chut mein hamesha ek aag si lagi rehti thi. Uska pati Abdullah bechara sirf tijarat aur safar karta rehta. Kabhi uski biwi ki pyasi chut ko thik se chodne ka naam bhi nahi leta tha.

Aaminah andar hi andar tadapti thi:
“Ya Khuda… kaisa namard shoahr baksha hai tune. Meri yeh moti sharamgah puri zindagi pyasi hi rahegi kya?”

Usne apne naram hatho se apni badi choochiyon ko dabaya, phir ungliyan neeche le jakar apni geeli chut ki pankhudiyon ko sehlaya. Thodi hi der mein uski chut se paani nikalne laga. Aur registan ki thand mein woh akele siskari bharte hue so gayi.

Aankhein band karte hi ek gehra, noorani sapna shuru ho gaya.

Achanak uske saamne ek bahut hi sundar, majboot jisam khada ho gaya.

Woh chilla padi: KON HO TUM... OR YAHA ANDAR KESE AA GAYE....

“Kaun ho tum?! Yahan andar kaise aa gaye?!”

Us shaksh ne dheere se muskurate hue kaha:
“Chillane se koi faida nahi, Aaminah. Main Jibreel hoon. Teri awaaz bahar nahi ja sakti. Yeh sab sirf tere aur mere beech hai.”

Aaminah ka dil zor-zor se dhadak raha tha. Jibreel uske paas aaya, uski badi choochiyon ko dekhte hue bola:

“Aaminah… tu duniya ki sabse badi, sabse umda aur sabse mukkadas Walida banne wali hai. Teri yeh bhara hua jism, yeh badi chhati jo dudh bahane ke liye tadap rahi hai aur yeh hamesha geeli rehne wali sharamgah...... sab kuch iske liye banaya gaya hai. Teri sharamgah mein ek aisa noor paida hoga jo saari duniya badal dega. Lekin iske liye tujhe badi mehnat karni padegi.”

Aaminah ghabra kar boli,
“Main? Mujh mein kya khasiyat hai? Mera shoahr toh mujhe chhuta bhi nahi… mera shoahr har waqt bahar hota hai aur isme mein haamila ho gayi to log kya kahenge?”

Jibreel hans pada.
“Sahi farmaya, isi wajah se tu chuni gayi hai. Tumhare shohar ko tumhe chhune ka mann nahi karta ye bhi ek khudai chaal hai, taaki tumhari bhook zyada bade aur samay aane par uska pura istemal ho. Teri chut hamesha geeli rehti hai – yahi tera maqsad hai. Ek nabi paida hoga tujhse… sabse saheel, sabse mukkadas. Jo aane wali duniya ka rukh badal dega. Ab duniya mein bas do nabi aane baki hain. Ek Muhammad aur dusra Imam Mahdi. Imam Mahdi ki paidaish mein abhi bahut deri hai unke liye sadiyaan beet jayengi. Par Muhammad ke aane ka paak saar maqam bahut jaldi hai. Isliye khuda ne tumse Muhammad ko janane se nawaza hai.

Lekin uske liye tujhe pehle teen purane nabiyon ka gadha maal apni sharamgah mein jama karna hoga – Adam, Moosa aur Isa. Unke noorani mani teri sharamgah mein daal kar rakhne hain.”

Aaminah ki aankhein chamak gayi. Woh ghabrate hue siskiya lete hue boli
“Main… main itna sab kaise jhel lungi? Main... main...”

Jibreel ne Aaminah ka gala dabakar usko uthaya aur kiss kiya. Jibreel ne apni jeeb Aaminah ke juban mein fasa di aur uski juban ke har ek kone ko chakh diya. Ye sab karte samay Aaminah ke aankhon se aansu aa gaye.

Jibreel ne use diwar par kas kar rakha tha, aur ye sab sunkar Aaminah ki sharamgah se dar ke mare garam pila paani behne laga.

“Sun le meri Noor-e-Aahira. Jitna bola utna kiya kar, agar tu ye nahi karegi to tumhari zindagi ka maqsad adhura reh jayega. Aurat hai mard se adha dimag rakhti hai to use istemal bhi utna hi kiya kar. Agar khuda ka qahar baras gaya to tu bas yahan raand ban kar reh jayegi. Aane jaane wale log tujh par thukenge, mutenge aur bas zarurat padne par raste par hi hambistari karenge wo bhi gine chune bhikari. Agar mere diye gaye deeni manzil par amal karegi to paise ki maali zaruratein puri hongi aur log tumhe nabi ki walida karke pukarenge, duniya bhar ka rutba kam pad jayega. Dar mat. Ye sab aisa hoga jaise gehre khwab mein tu maze le rahi ho.
Tu neem-behoshi ki halat mein rahegi, mehsoos karegi har cheez, lekin qabu nahi hoga.”

Jibreel ne uske kaan mein fusa-fusa kar kaha:
"Jab tum pichle 3 nabiyon ki gadhi malai apni chut mein jama kar degi tab agle Jumme ke din tumhe mere saath hambistari karni hogi. Tab main apna gadha maal teri sharamgah mein dakhil kar dunga jo sab nabiyon ke maal ko gond ki tarah jod kar rakhega.

Agar tu sab maal ikattha kar li toh tu pure 2 saal ke liye haamila rahegi.
Aur tu duniya ki sabse mazhabi aur sabse badi paak saar ammi banegi.”

Aaminah ki sharmgah sapne mein hi geeli hone lagi. Uske raano ke darmiyan gilawat fail ke behne lagi thi. Woh darr bhi rahi thi lekin andar se uski jismani bhuk jag rahi thi.

Usne poocha, “Aur mera pati Abdullah?” Jibreel muskuraya, “Woh bebas shohar toh pehle hi marne wala hai. Tera beta uske baad paida hoga… taaki duniya dekhe ki uska asli baap kaun hai. Tujhe 2 saal bewah hokar duniya ki sabse umda walida ban kar dikhana hoga.”

Aaminah ka dil zor se dhadka. Aur woh bistar par sapne se jaag kar uth gayi. Uske jhopdi ka darwaza ab bhi khula tha, uske gale par abhi bhi majboot hatho ke nishan the aur uski raano ke darmiyan abhi bhi paani beh raha tha.
```
