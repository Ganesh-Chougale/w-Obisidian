## A. What is Investment (core idea)

- You give money today
- To get **more money in future**

That “more money” comes from:

* growth
* interest
* business profit

---

## B. The `3` Layers of Investment (big picture)

### Layer 1: `Asset Class` (WHERE money goes)

This is the **foundation**

* **Equity** → ownership in companies (stocks, equity mutual funds)
* **Debt** → lending money (FD, bonds, debt funds)
* **Commodity** → store of value (Gold, Silver, Cruid Oil, Natural gas, Agricultural products)
* **Real Estate** → land/property

---

### Layer 2: Investment `Vehicle` (WHAT exactly you use)

This is the **container**

* Stocks (direct equity)
* Mutual Funds
* ETFs
* PPF / EPF
* Bonds

- Example:

* Equity (asset) → Mutual Fund (vehicle)

---

### Layer 3: Investment Method (HOW you invest)

This is the **approach**

* Lump Sum
* SIP
* Flexible / Top-up

---

## C. How everything connects

- Example:

* Asset → Equity
* Vehicle → Mutual Fund
* Method → SIP

- Full sentence:

> “I invest monthly (SIP) in equity mutual funds”

---

## D. Risk vs Return (core rule)

| Type   | Risk   | Return |
| ------ | ------ | ------ |
| Debt   | Low    | Low    |
| Equity | High   | High   |
| Hybrid | Medium | Medium |

- Rule:
**Higher return = higher risk**

---

## E. Time Horizon (very important)

* Short term (0–3 yrs) → safer assets (debt)
* Medium (3–5 yrs) → balanced
* Long term (5+ yrs) → equity works best

---

## F. Power drivers of investing

### 1. Compounding

Money earning money

### 2. Consistency

Regular investing (SIP)

### 3. Time

Longest time = biggest growth

---

## G. Biggest mistakes

* Chasing quick profit
* No consistency
* Ignoring risk
* Starting late
