## Approach 
01. The observation — what you noticed
02. A real-life story — your own experience
03. The pattern — where else you see it
04. Your hypothesis — why you think humans behave this way
05. Examples — work, exercise, learning, relationships, etc.
06. Counterexamples — situations where it doesn't apply
07. Psychology research — see whether existing research supports/contradicts it
08. Your interpretation — what you think the research means
09. Practical application — how people can use the idea
10. Your conclusion — your overall model/idea

### Level 1 — Publishing:
You put your work somewhere publicly accessible.
People can read it.

### Level 2 — Credibility:
Your work has some form of professional/academic .
For example:
- editorial review
- peer review
- conference publication
- citations
- recognized publication platforms
- collaboration with researchers
- professional organizations


### Level 3 — Recognition:
Other people begin saying “Have you read X's work on this?”
or citing your work, discussing it, building upon it, inviting you to contribute, etc.
