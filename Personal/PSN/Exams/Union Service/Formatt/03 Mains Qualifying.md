# 2. Mains – Qualifying Papers Format

## 2A1. Paper A – Indian Language

* **Type:** Descriptive (written)
* **Total Marks:** 300
* **Duration:** 3 hours
* **Qualifying Nature:** Mandatory to pass
* **Negative Marking:** ❌ No

### Paper structure:

* **Comprehension**

  * Passage + questions
  * Tests understanding ability

* **Precis Writing**

  * Summarize given passage

* **Translation**

  * English ↔ Chosen Indian Language

* **Grammar & Usage**

  * Vocabulary, sentence correction, usage

* **Essay Writing**

  * Short essay/topic-based writing

### Language options:

Candidates choose one language from the Eighth Schedule languages such as:

* Marathi
* Hindi
* Gujarati
* Kannada
* Tamil
* Telugu
* Bengali
* Urdu
* Punjabi
* etc.

(Except candidates from certain North-Eastern states who may get exemption as per UPSC rules.)

---

## 2A2. Paper B – English

* **Type:** Descriptive (written)
* **Total Marks:** 300
* **Duration:** 3 hours
* **Qualifying Nature:** Mandatory to pass
* **Negative Marking:** ❌ No

### Paper structure:

* **Comprehension**

  * Passage + questions

* **Precis Writing**

  * Summary writing

* **Grammar & Usage**

  * Error correction, vocabulary, sentence structure

* **Short Essay**

  * Topic-based writing

* **Language Skills**

  * Clarity, coherence, expression

---

## Important clarity

* No MCQs ❌
* Answers must be written in paragraph/descriptive form
* Both papers are only qualifying
* Marks are NOT added in final merit
* If candidate fails either paper:

  * Remaining merit papers are not evaluated

---

## Simple structure view

* Paper A → Basic proficiency in chosen Indian language
* Paper B → Basic English proficiency check

Focus:

* Clarity
* Grammar
* Expression
* Comprehension
* Formal writing ability
