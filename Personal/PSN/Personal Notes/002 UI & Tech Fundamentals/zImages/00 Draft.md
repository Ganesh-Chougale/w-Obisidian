# Visual & Technical — Learning Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Price Foundation

Start only with **price**, nothing else.

### 1. Candlestick Structure

Understand what one candle means.

You must learn:

* Open
* Close
* High
* Low
* Body
* Wick / Shadow

Goal:

> Read market sentiment from a single candle.

---

### 2. Trend Identification

Markets move in **3 directions**

1. Uptrend
2. Downtrend
3. Sideways

Key rule:

| Trend     | Structure                |
| --------- | ------------------------ |
| Uptrend   | Higher High + Higher Low |
| Downtrend | Lower High + Lower Low   |

Goal:

> Ability to look at a chart and instantly say the trend.

---

### 3. Candlestick Patterns

Only learn **important patterns** (avoid pattern overload).

Start with:

Bullish

* Hammer
* Bullish Engulfing

Bearish

* Shooting Star
* Bearish Engulfing

Neutral

* Doji

Goal:

> Understand **reversal signals**.

---

# Phase 2 — Market Structure

### 4. Support & Resistance

These are **price reaction zones**.

Support
→ Price tends to bounce upward.

Resistance
→ Price tends to fall.

Example:

```
Resistance
---------
price moving
---------
Support
```

Goal:

> Identify where buyers and sellers exist.

---

### 5. Breakout & Breakdown

Breakout
→ Price moves **above resistance**

Breakdown
→ Price moves **below support**

Goal:

> Understand when a stock starts a **new move**.

---

### 6. Fake Breakout (Trap)

Very important concept.

Sometimes price:

1. Breaks resistance
2. Traders enter
3. Price falls back

This is called a **fake breakout** or **bull trap**.

Goal:

> Avoid getting trapped.

---

# Phase 3 — Volume

Volume shows **participation**.

### 7. Volume Basics

Volume = number of shares traded.

Interpretation:

| Price   | Volume   | Meaning     |
| ------- | -------- | ----------- |
| Price ↑ | Volume ↑ | Strong move |
| Price ↑ | Volume ↓ | Weak move   |

Goal:

> Know whether move is **real or weak**.

---

### 8. Volume Confirmation

A breakout should have:

```
Resistance break
+
High Volume
```

If volume is low → breakout may fail.

Goal:

> Validate breakouts.

---

# Phase 4 — Indicators

Indicators come **after price and volume**.

Never start trading with indicators first.

Use indicators available on **GoCharting**

### 9. Moving Averages

Two types:

SMA
→ Simple Moving Average

EMA
→ Exponential Moving Average

Purpose:

* Trend direction
* Dynamic support/resistance

Common values:

```
20
50
200
```

---

### 10. MACD

MACD = Momentum indicator.

Purpose:

* Trend strength
* Momentum shift
* Crossovers

---

### 11. ADX

ADX measures **trend strength**, not direction.

Range:

| ADX Value | Meaning           |
| --------- | ----------------- |
| <20       | Weak trend        |
| 20–40     | Strong trend      |
| >40       | Very strong trend |
