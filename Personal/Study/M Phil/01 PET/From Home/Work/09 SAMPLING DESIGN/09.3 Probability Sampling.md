## 9.3 Probability Sampling
`Probability Sampling`: every population unit has a known chance of being selected. Selection is based on randomness, not personal choice.
- example: from 10,000 university students, randomly select 500 students so each student has a known chance of selection.  

### Main types
`Simple Random Sampling`: everyone has an equal chance.  
- example: randomly pick 500 student IDs.  
`Systematic Sampling`: select every nth*- person after a random starting point.  
- example: select every 20th student from a list.  
`Stratified Sampling`: divide the population into groups (strata || level || स्तर), then sample from each group.  
- example: separately sample students from Engineering, Arts, and Commerce.  
`Cluster Sampling`: divide the population into natural groups (clusters), then randomly select groups.  
- example: randomly select 5 colleges and study students within them.  
Mental model: `Probability = Random selection + Known chance`  
Exam yield:- Know the 4 types + their basic difference.
