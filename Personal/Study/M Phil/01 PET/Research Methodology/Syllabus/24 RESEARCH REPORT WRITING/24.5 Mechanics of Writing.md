## 24.5 Mechanics of Writing

`Mechanics of Writing`: the **rules and practical details** that make research writing clear, correct, and properly presented.

It mainly includes:
- `Grammar & Spelling` → use correct language.
- `Punctuation` → use commas, full stops, etc. correctly.
- `Sentence Structure` → write clear and understandable sentences.
- `Paragraphing` → organise related ideas together.
- `Formatting` → use proper headings, spacing, tables, figures, and references.

- example: Instead of writing a long, unclear sentence, divide it into shorter sentences and use proper headings to make the research report easier to read.
