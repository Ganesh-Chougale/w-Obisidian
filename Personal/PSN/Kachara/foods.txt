**Final answer:**
**Fibre and carbohydrates are types of nutrients.** Fibre is actually a **special type of carbohydrate**, and there are **other nutrient groups like these** that your body uses for energy, structure, and regulation.

---

## 1️⃣ What is **Carbohydrates**?

**Carbohydrates (carbs)** are nutrients that the body mainly uses for **energy**.

**What happens in the body:**

* Carbs are broken down into **glucose (sugar)**
* Glucose enters the blood → raises blood sugar
* Insulin helps move glucose into cells for energy

**Examples:**

* Rice, roti, bread
* Sugar, jaggery
* Potato
* Fruits

**Types of carbohydrates:**

* **Simple carbs** → digest fast (sugar, white rice)
* **Complex carbs** → digest slower (whole grains, legumes)

---

## 2️⃣ What is **Fibre**?

**Fibre is a type of carbohydrate**, but **your body cannot digest it**.

**What happens in the body:**

* Fibre does **not turn into glucose**
* Slows digestion
* Slows sugar absorption
* Feeds good gut bacteria

**Why fibre is important:**

* Prevents blood sugar spikes
* Improves digestion
* Helps with fullness (less overeating)
* Supports heart health

**Examples:**

* Vegetables
* Whole fruits
* Whole grains
* Pulses
* Seeds

---

## 3️⃣ Key difference (very important)

| Feature             | Carbohydrates | Fibre    |
| ------------------- | ------------- | -------- |
| Digested?           | Yes           | ❌ No     |
| Raises blood sugar? | Yes           | ❌ No     |
| Gives energy?       | Yes           | Indirect |
| Slows digestion?    | ❌ No          | ✅ Yes    |

---

## 4️⃣ Other nutrient types “like this” (exists in food)

### 🥩 **Protein**

* Builds muscles, hormones, enzymes
* Slows digestion
* Helps repair body tissues
* Examples: dal, paneer, eggs, fish, meat

---

### 🧈 **Fats**

* Long-term energy
* Helps absorb vitamins
* Keeps you full longer
* Examples: ghee, oil, nuts, seeds

---

### 💧 **Water**

* Essential for all body functions
* No calories

---

### 🧂 **Vitamins & Minerals**

* Help reactions in the body
* Do not give energy
* Examples:

  * Iron (spinach)
  * Calcium (milk)
  * Vitamin C (citrus fruits)

---

## 5️⃣ Simple way to remember

* **Carbs = energy**
* **Fibre = control**
* **Protein = build**
* **Fat = store & support**
* **Vitamins/minerals = regulate**
