## 22.6 R-Type & Q-Type Analysis

`R-Type Analysis`: studies **relationships among variables**.  
- example: examining the relationship between `study time`, `attendance`, and `exam marks`.

`Q-Type Analysis`: studies **similarities among cases, people, or respondents**.  
- example: grouping students who have similar `study habits, attendance, and exam performance`.

`R-Type` → **Variables ↔ Variables**  
`Q-Type` → **Person/Case ↔ Person/Case**
