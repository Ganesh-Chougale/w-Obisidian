# 3. Mains – Merit Papers Format
## 3A. Essay Paper

* **Type:** Descriptive
* **Total Marks:** 250
* **Duration:** 3 hours
* **Negative Marking:** ❌ No

### Paper structure:

* Usually **2–3 essays to be written**
* Topics from:

  * Social issues
  * Economy
  * Politics
  * Ethics / philosophy
  * Current affairs

Marks depend on:

* Structure (intro–body–conclusion)
* Clarity of thought
* Examples & balance

---

## 3B. General Studies 1 (GS1)

* **Type:** Descriptive

* **Duration:** 3 hours

* Geography

* Mix of:

  * Short answers (10–15 marks)

## Important clarity

* All papers are **written (no MCQs)**
* Answers must be **structured (intro + body + conclusion)**
* **Word limit matters** (usually mentioned in paper)
* **Marks ARE counted in final merit**
