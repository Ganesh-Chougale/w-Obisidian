## 26.2 Computer Technology

`Computer Technology`: the technologies used to **store, process, analyze, and communicate research data and information**.

- `Data Storage` → databases, hard drives, cloud storage.
- `Data Processing` → computers and software that process large amounts of data.
- `Data Analysis` → statistical and data-analysis software.
- `Communication` → internet, email, online platforms, and video conferencing.

- example: A researcher collects survey responses online, stores them in a database, analyzes them using statistical software, and shares the findings through a digital report.

`Collect / Store` → `Process` → `Analyze` → `Communicate` → `Research Information`
