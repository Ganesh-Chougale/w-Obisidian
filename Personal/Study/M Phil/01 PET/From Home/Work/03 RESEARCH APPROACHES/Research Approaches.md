## 3.1 Quantitative
`Quantitative`: studies something using numbers, measurements, and statistics.  
- example: new flagship phone has over 10k positive reviews. & most of them are 4/5 stars  
## 3.2 Qualitative
`Qualitative`: studies experiences, opinions, meanings, and reasons using words or observations instead of numbers.  
- example: reading those actual reviews & knowing user liked the camera, screen, performance etc.    

`Quantitative` → Numbers → Measure → Compare    
`Qualitative` → Words → Understand → Why / How
