# 4. Personality Test (Interview) Format

Conducted by Union Public Service Commission

## Interview / Personality Test

* **Type:** Face-to-face interview
* **Total Marks:** 275
* **Duration:** ~25–45 minutes
* **Negative Marking:** ❌ No

---

## What they evaluate:

* **Decision Making**

  * Administrative judgment
  * Situational handling
  * Problem-solving ability

* **Communication**

  * Clarity of expression
  * Confidence
  * Logical articulation

* **Awareness**

  * National + international current affairs
  * Social, political, economic issues
  * Your educational background
  * Home state, hobbies, work profile

* **Personality**

  * Attitude
  * Honesty
  * Balance of opinion
  * Presence of mind
  * Emotional maturity

* **Leadership & Administrative Aptitude**

  * Ability to handle responsibility
  * Public service orientation

---

## Question nature:

* Strongly based on your **DAF (Detailed Application Form)**
* Mix of:

  * Opinion-based questions
  * Real-life administrative scenarios
  * Current affairs discussions
  * Ethical dilemmas
  * Background-related questions

---

## Board structure

* Usually conducted by a panel chaired by a UPSC board member
* Around 4–5 panel members ask questions
* Interview atmosphere is generally formal but conversational

---

## Important clarity

* No fixed syllabus ❌
* No factual mug-up test alone
* Board evaluates:

  * How you think
  * How you react
  * How balanced and mature your views are
* Marks are directly added in final merit

---

## Simple structure view

* Not a pure knowledge test → Personality assessment
* Focus:

  * Thinking
  * Judgment
  * Communication
  * Administrative suitability
  * Emotional balance
