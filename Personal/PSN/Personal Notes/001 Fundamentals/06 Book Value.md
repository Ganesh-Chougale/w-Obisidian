# `Book Value`

### Book Value = `what the company is worth on paper after paying all debts`

```
<!-- in my words -->
in last case if company sold everything + paid all the loans of finiancial burden on it, after that how much investor gets per share
```

Simple meaning:

```text
Company Assets − Company Liabilities = Net Worth
```

That **net worth per share** is called **Book Value**.

---

## Formula

Book Value Per Share = (Total Assets − Total Liabilities) / Total Shares

Meaning:

| Term              | Meaning                         |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Total Assets      | everything the company owns     |
| Total Liabilities | everything the company owes     |
| Total Shares      | number of shares investors hold |

---

# Simple example

Assume a company has:

| Item                      | Value      |
| ------------------------- | ---------- |
| Factories, cash, machines | ₹100 crore |
| Loans & debts             | ₹40 crore  |

Company net worth:

```text
100 − 40 = ₹60 crore
```

If the company has **3 crore shares**:
60/3 = 20  
```text
Book Value per share = ₹20
```

---

# What Book Value tries to answer

```text
If the company shut down today,
sold everything,
paid all debts,
how much money per share would remain?
```

That remaining value ≈ **Book Value**.

---

# Example with stock price

| Item        | Value |
| ----------- | ----- |
| Share Price | ₹120  |
| Book Value  | ₹40   |

Meaning:

```text
Investors pay ₹120
for assets worth ₹40 per share
```

The market believes the company is worth **more than just its assets**.

---

# Why market price is often higher than Book Value

Because investors expect:

* future profits
* brand value
* growth
* competitive advantage

Example:

* software companies
* consumer brands
* tech companies

Their real value is **not just physical assets**.

---

# When Book Value becomes important

It matters more in **asset-heavy industries**:

| Industry      | Reason       |
| ------------- | ------------ |
| Banks         | money assets |
| Manufacturing | factories    |
| Real estate   | land value   |

Here assets matter a lot.

---

# Very important warning ⚠️

```text
Book Value ≠ real market value
```

Because:

* some assets lose value
* some assets are outdated
* some assets are undervalued

Accounting numbers are **not perfect reality**.

---

# Simple mental model

```text
Market Cap   → company size
Current Price → last traded price
High / Low    → yearly price range
Stock P/E     → price vs profit
Book Value    → assets minus debt per share
```
