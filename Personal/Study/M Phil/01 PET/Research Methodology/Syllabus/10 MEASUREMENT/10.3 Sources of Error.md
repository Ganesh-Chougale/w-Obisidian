## 10.3 Sources of Error
`Measurement Error`: the difference between the value actually measured and the true value.  
- example: a student's actual satisfaction is `4/5`, but due to a confusing question they report `3/5`.  

### Main sources
`Respondent Error`: participant gives an inaccurate answer.  
- example: student does not remember how often they used GEN-AI.  
`Researcher Error`: researcher influences or records the response incorrectly.  
- example: researcher unintentionally leads participants toward a particular answer.  
`Instrument Error`: questionnaire, scale, or measuring tool is faulty or unclear.  
- example: a confusing question produces inconsistent answers.  
`Sampling Error`: the selected sample differs from the population in a way that affects the result.  
- example: studying only highly active GEN-AI users when the population includes all users.  
Mental model:  
`True value → Measurement process → Error → Observed value`  
Exam yield: Remember the major sources: Respondent + Researcher + Instrument + Sampling.
