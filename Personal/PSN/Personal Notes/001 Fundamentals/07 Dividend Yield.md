# `Dividend Yield`

### Dividend Yield = `cash return you receive from a stock every year`

Companies sometimes **share part of their profit with shareholders**.
That cash payment is called a **Dividend**.

Dividend Yield tells:

```text
How much cash return you get
compared to the stock price
```

---

# Formula

```text
Dividend Yield = (Dividend Per Share / Share Price) × 100
```

Where:

| Term               | Meaning                             |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Dividend Per Share | cash paid to you per share per year |
| Share Price        | current market price of the share   |

---

# Simple example

Assume:

| Item               | Value |
| ------------------ | ----- |
| Share Price        | ₹200  |
| Dividend per share | ₹10   |

Calculation:

```text
Dividend Yield = (10 / 200) × 100
Dividend Yield = 5%
```

Meaning:

```text
For every ₹100 invested,
you receive ₹5 per year as dividend.
```

---

# Another example

| Share Price | Dividend | Yield |
| ----------- | -------- | ----- |
| ₹500        | ₹10      | 2%    |
| ₹500        | ₹25      | 5%    |
| ₹500        | ₹50      | 10%   |

Higher dividend → higher yield.

---

# Important insight

Dividend Yield depends on **two things**:

```text
1. Dividend amount
2. Share price
```

If price falls but dividend stays same → **yield increases**.

Example:

| Price | Dividend | Yield |
| ----- | -------- | ----- |
| ₹200  | ₹10      | 5%    |
| ₹100  | ₹10      | 10%   |

Dividend did not change, but yield doubled.

---

# Why some companies pay high dividends

Usually:

* mature businesses
* stable cash flow
* slower growth

Examples industries:

* utilities
* banks
* FMCG

These companies already grew large, so they **return cash to shareholders**.

---

# Why some companies pay low or zero dividend

Growth companies often **reinvest profits** instead.

Money goes into:

* expansion
* R&D
* new markets
* acquisitions

Example sectors:

* technology
* startups
* fast-growing companies

---

# Important warning ⚠️

```text
Very high dividend yield can be risky
```

Sometimes yield becomes high because:

```text
Stock price crashed
```

So always check **why** the yield is high.

---

# Simple mental model

```text
Market Cap   → company size
Current Price → last trade price
High / Low    → yearly price range
Stock P/E     → price vs profit
Book Value    → net worth per share
Dividend Yield → cash return from stock
```
