## 19 Chi-Square Test
`Chi-Square Test`: A statistical test mainly used for **categorical data** to determine whether observed frequencies differ from expected frequencies or whether two categorical variables are associated.  
* **Goodness of Fit** → checks whether observed frequencies match expected frequencies.  
* **Test of Association/Independence** → checks whether two categorical variables are related.  

Example:  
`Gender` × `Preference for Gen-AI Response` → Chi-Square Test → `Associated / Not Associated`  

Remember: **Chi-Square = frequencies/counts + categorical data.**
