## 23.1 Meaning of Interpretation

`Interpretation`: explaining **what the research results mean** and what can be understood from them.

It goes beyond simply presenting the data or results.

- example: The study found that students who study more hours generally score higher.  
  - `Result` → More study time is associated with higher marks.  
  - `Interpretation` → Regular study may help students perform better in exams.

`Research Results` → `Meaning / Explanation` → `Understanding`
