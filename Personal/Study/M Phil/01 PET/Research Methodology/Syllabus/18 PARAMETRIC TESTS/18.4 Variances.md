## 18.4 Variances
`Variance`: Measures **how spread out the data values are around their mean**.  

`Tests of Variances`: Statistical tests used to determine whether a population variance equals a specified value or whether **two population variances differ**.  

- example: Two machines produce parts.  
  * `Machine A` → measurements have low variation.  
  * `Machine B` → measurements have high variation.  
  * A variance test can determine whether their **population variability is significantly different**.  

### Common comparisons
- `One Variance` → compare a population variance with a specified value.  
- `Two Variances` → compare the variances of two populations.  

`Data` → `Measure Spread (Variance)` → `Compare Variances` → `Decision`  

### Remember

**Mean tells us the centre.**  
**Variance tells us the spread.**  
**Tests of variances = “Is the amount of variability really different?”**
