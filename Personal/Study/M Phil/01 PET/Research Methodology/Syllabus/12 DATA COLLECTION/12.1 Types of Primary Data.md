## 12.1.1 Observation
`Observation`: collecting data by watching behaviour/events and recording what happens.  
- example: You observe how people use a GEN-AI tool and record how often they provide additional context.  
Key idea: `Watch → Record`  
---

## 12.1.2 Interview
`Interview`: collecting data by directly asking respondents questions and recording their answers.  
- example: You ask 20 developers, “Does giving more context improve your GEN-AI results?” and record their responses.  
Key idea: `Ask → Listen → Record`  
---

## 12.1.3 Questionnaire
`Questionnaire`: a structured set of questions that respondents themselves answer, usually in written or digital form.  
- example: You send a Google Form containing 15 questions about users' experience with GEN-AI.  
Key idea: `Give questions → Respondent fills them`  
---

## 12.1.4 Schedule
`Schedule`: a structured set of questions where an investigator/enumerator asks the questions and fills in the answers.  
- example: You visit 50 people, ask each person the same 15 questions, and you fill the form based on their answers.  

Key difference:  

`Questionnaire` → Respondent fills it  
`Schedule` → Investigator fills it  

### Easy memory

`Observation` → Watch  
`Interview` → Ask  
`Questionnaire` → Respondent writes   
`Schedule` → Investigator writes
