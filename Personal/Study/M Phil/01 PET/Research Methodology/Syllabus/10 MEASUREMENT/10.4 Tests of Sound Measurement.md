## 10.4 Tests of Sound Measurement
`Sound Measurement`: a measurement should give consistent results and actually measure what it is supposed to measure.
### 2 Core Tests
`Reliability`: checks consistency — does the measurement give similar results when repeated under similar conditions?  
- example: a GEN-AI satisfaction questionnaire gives nearly similar results when the same students answer it again.  
`Validity`: checks accuracy — does the measurement actually measure what it claims to measure?  
- example: a questionnaire intended to measure GEN-AI response quality should actually measure response quality, not students' general computer skills.  
Mental model:  
`Reliability = Consistent?`  
`Validity = Correct thing?`  
Exam yield:  
`Reliability → Consistency`  
`Validity → Accuracy / What it is supposed to measure`
