# 1. Preliminary Examination – Paper Format

Conducted by Union Public Service Commission
## 1A. General Studies Paper I (GS)

* **Questions:** 100 MCQs
* **Marks per question:** 2
* **Total Marks:** 200
* **Duration:** 2 hours

### Question nature:

* History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern)
* Indian National Movement
* Geography (India + World)
* Indian Polity & Governance
* Economy
* Environment & Ecology
* Science & Technology
* Current Affairs
* International Relations

### Marking:

* Correct → +2
* Wrong → **-0.66 (1/3 negative)**
* Unattempted → 0

---

## 1B. CSAT (General Studies Paper II)

* **Questions:** 80 MCQs
* **Marks per question:** 2.5
* **Total Marks:** 200
* **Duration:** 2 hours
* **Type:** *Qualifying (need 33% = 66 marks)*

### Question nature:

* Reading Comprehension
* Logical Reasoning
* Analytical Ability
* Decision Making
* Basic Numeracy
* Data Interpretation
* Mental Ability

### Marking:

* Correct → +2.5
* Wrong → **-0.83 (1/3 negative)**
* Unattempted → 0

---

## Important clarity

* Both papers are **objective (OMR-based MCQs)**
* **Language:** English & Hindi
* GS Paper I marks decide the cutoff
* CSAT is qualifying only, but many candidates fail here

---

## Simple structure view

* Paper 1 → Knowledge-heavy
* Paper 2 → Aptitude & comprehension-based

---

## Additional UPSC Prelims Notes

* Candidates approximately **12–13 times** the number of vacancies qualify for Mains
* Prelims marks are **not added** in final merit
* Only Mains + Interview decide final rank
* UPSC Prelims is considered one of the toughest elimination stages in India
