## 18.5 Correlation Coefficients
`Correlation Coefficient`: A number that shows the **direction and strength of the relationship between two variables**.  
`Test of Correlation`: Determines whether the observed correlation in a sample is **statistically significant** in the population.  
- example: We study `Study Hours` and `Exam Scores`.  
  * Sample correlation `r = +0.70`.
  * The test checks whether this relationship is likely to exist in the population or could have occurred by chance.

### Remember
* `r > 0` → Positive relationship
* `r < 0` → Negative relationship
* `r ≈ 0` → Little/no linear relationship
* `|r|` closer to `1` → Stronger relationship
`Sample Correlation` → `Statistical Test` → `Significant / Not Significant`  

**Important:** `Correlation ≠ Causation` — a relationship between two variables does not by itself prove that one causes the other.
