## 22.5 Factor Rotation

`Factor Rotation`: a technique used after factor analysis to **rearrange the factors so that their meaning becomes easier to understand**.

It makes variables more clearly associated with particular factors.

- example: After factor analysis, `camera, display, battery, and performance` are spread across several factors. Rotation makes the pattern clearer, such as:
  - `Camera + Display` → **User Experience**
  - `Battery + Performance` → **Device Performance**

`Initial Factors` → `Rotate` → `Clearer Factor Structure` → `Easier Interpretation`
