
# 4. Interview (Personality Test) Format
## Interview

* **Type:** Face-to-face interview
* **Total Marks:** 275
* **Duration:** ~20–40 minutes
* **Negative Marking:** ❌ No

---

## What they evaluate:

* **Decision Making**

  * Situational questions
  * Administrative judgment

* **Communication**

  * Clarity of speech
  * Confidence + articulation

* **Awareness**

  * Current affairs
  * State (Maharashtra) + national issues
  * Your background (education, place, etc.)

* **Personality**

  * Attitude
  * Honesty
  * Balance of opinion
  * Presence of mind

---

## Question nature:

* Based on your **DAF (Detailed Application Form)**
* Mix of:

  * Opinion-based questions
  * Real-life scenarios
  * Basic knowledge + current issues

---

## Important clarity

* No fixed syllabus ❌
* No right/wrong like written exam
* Panel checks **how you think, not what you memorize**
* Marks **directly added to final merit**

---

## Simple structure view

* Not a knowledge test → **Personality test**
* Focus → **Thinking + behavior + communication**
