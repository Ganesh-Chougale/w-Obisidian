### `What is computer programming`?  
- Computer programming is an `attempt` to get computer to complete a specific task without any mistake.  
real life example  
```
lets say you want your dumb friend to build a lego set but that friend ain't do a step unless you give him instruction (each & every instruction).

but your dumb frined is far from intelligence on his own, so you must give him exact instruction on how to build it.

if there is a even one piece wrong the entire lego set will be ruined.

here, 
replace your dumb friend with computer.
the human language(Marathi, Hindi, English) with programming language(Assembly, C/C++, Java, Python, JS, C#).
the processs of telling your friend instructions is Coding in CS case.

the only difference between your friend & computer is Computers are very well in doing repitative task at larger scale(based on CPU/GPU).

the one more problem with communicating with computer is they dont understand human language or even programming language, they only understand machine code & more likely binaries (0s & 1s)
```  
`English` ➝ `Programming Language` ➝ `Compiler/Interpreter` ➝ `Machine Code/Byte Code` ➝ `Binaries`
