### Sumation

> `Goal`: Summation means addition of numbers

**Concept:** summation means addition of numbers.

<details>
<summary>Formula & notation</summary>

$$
a+b=c
$$

- \(a\): First value
- \(b\): Second value
- \(c\): calculated value

</details>

<details>
<summary>Example</summary>

**Problem:** Calculate \(2+3\).

$$
2+3=5
$$

**Answer:** \(5\)

</details>
