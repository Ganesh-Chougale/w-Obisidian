# 2.3 Quantitative vs Qualitative Research

## Core Difference

The simplest distinction:

> **Quantitative research → Numbers and measurement.**
> **Qualitative research → Meanings, experiences, and descriptions.**

---

# 1. Quantitative Research

### Meaning

**Quantitative research deals with measurable variables and numerical data.**

It asks questions such as:

* How much?
* How many?
* How often?
* How strongly?
* Is there a measurable relationship?

### Our Example

Research topic:

> **The effect of contextual information on the quality of GEN-AI responses.**

A quantitative study could give Gen-AI the same task with different amounts of context and assign each response a **quality score**.

Example:

```text
Context Level     Response Quality
----------------------------------
Low               5/10
Medium            7/10
High              8/10
```

The researcher can then statistically analyse whether differences in context are associated with differences in response quality.

### Typical Characteristics

* Numerical data
* Measurable variables
* Structured data collection
* Statistical analysis
* Larger samples are often used

---

# 2. Qualitative Research

### Meaning

**Qualitative research focuses on meanings, experiences, perceptions, opinions, behaviours, and detailed descriptions.**

It asks questions such as:

* Why?
* How?
* What does this experience mean?
* How do people perceive something?

### Our Example

Instead of assigning only scores, we could interview users and ask:

> **"What kind of context do you believe helps Gen-AI understand your request better, and why?"**

We might discover responses such as:

> "I usually explain the purpose of the task because otherwise the AI gives generic answers."

The researcher analyses these responses to identify **themes, patterns, and meanings**.

### Typical Characteristics

* Words/text rather than primarily numbers
* Experiences and perceptions
* Open-ended questions
* Interviews, observations, discussions, etc.
* Thematic or interpretive analysis

---

# Key Difference

| Quantitative           | Qualitative                          |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Numbers**            | **Words / meanings**                 |
| Measures               | Explores understanding               |
| "How much/how many?"   | "Why/how?"                           |
| Structured             | Often more open-ended                |
| Statistical analysis   | Thematic/interpretive analysis       |
| Variables are measured | Experiences/perceptions are explored |

---

# Same Research Topic — Two Approaches

### Quantitative

> **Does increasing relevant contextual information produce a measurable increase in Gen-AI response quality?**

```text
Context → Quality Score → Statistical Analysis
```

### Qualitative

> **How do users experience and understand the role of contextual information when interacting with Gen-AI?**

```text
User Experiences → Themes → Interpretation
```

---

# Important: They Can Be Combined

A study does **not** always have to choose only one.

A **mixed-methods** study combines quantitative and qualitative approaches.

For example:

```text
Quantitative
    ↓
Measure whether context changes response quality

+

Qualitative
    ↓
Understand why users or researchers observe those changes
```

This can provide both **measurement and deeper understanding**.
