Inner Maharashtra Visits

1. 05 to 24 January Window
- Winter still pleasant (No extreme Heat or Rain)
- after 10 because post New Year rush gone
- before 24 because republic date.

2. 20 to 30 June Window
- Monsoon has started → casual tourists drop
- Roads quieter except weekends
- Pre Manson crowd is still visible but still nothing compare to fully fledge Manson crowd.

3. 05 - 15 September Window
- Post-Monsoon Beauty Peak, Pleasant weather, Green scenery
- Ganapathi Festival so few tourist.

Tips: 
- within all windows. choose Tuesday, Wednesday (मंगळवार, बुधवार)
- based on destination revise the dates again.
