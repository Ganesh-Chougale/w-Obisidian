## 22.2 Classification

`Classification`: grouping multivariate techniques based on **what they are used for** and **how they mathematically work**.

Commonly, techniques can be grouped by their purpose:

- `Dependence techniques` → study how one or more **dependent variables** are affected by other variables.  
  - example: studying how `study time + attendance` affect `exam marks`.
- `Interdependence techniques` → study relationships among variables **without treating one as the main outcome**.  
  - example: finding groups of related factors among `price, quality, brand image, and customer satisfaction`.
