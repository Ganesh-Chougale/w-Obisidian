1. Controll Sticky Dandruff
use shampoo which contains
- Ketoconazole
OR
- Zinc Pyrithione
OR
- Selenium Sulfide

this balance Malassezia fungus on scalp.

2. while cleaning: no aggressive friction like sharp scrub, hot water, nails. curly hair + inflamed scalp dislike this.

3. while handling hair:
- no for tiny gap comb, hard combing on dry hair.
- yes for gentle massage while washing, pat drying, wide-tooth comb. 
- master pat drying instead of rubbing towel aggressively.

4. Nutrition Intake
- Protein : Hair is protein-heavy tissue.
- Zinc : Important for follicles/scalp.
- Iron : Low iron can worsen diffuse shedding.
- Omega-3 : Inflammation support.
- Vitamin D : Very commonly low in Indians.

5. Oil Cycle
Rosemary Oil 10% & Coconut Oil 90%
this mixture apply gently on scalp 2 times a week.
after 30 mins to 2 hours wash it with shampoo
