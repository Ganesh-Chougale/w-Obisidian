# `Market Cap` : Market Capitalization  
### Market Cap = `how big the company is in the stock market’s eyes`  
It is the market's opinion value.  

Market Cap like the price of an entire shop 🏪  
- One item price = `Share Price`
- Total items = `Total Shares`
- Shop value = `Market Cap`

⚠️ If item price(Share Price) changes → shop value(Market Cap) changes,  
Even though nothing inside the shop changed  

### `Types of Caps` (India context 🇮🇳)  
| Category         | Market Cap (Approx) | Meaning / Size                 | Risk Level | Volatility | Growth Potential | Best Suited For                             |
| ---------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------- | ---------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 🐘 **Large Cap** | Top 100 companies   | Biggest, most stable companies | Low        | Low        | Moderate         | Peace-of-mind, long-term investors          |
| 🐎 **Mid Cap**   | Next 150 companies  | Growing, well-established      | Medium     | Medium     | High             | Balanced growth + risk seekers              |
| 🐣 **Small Cap** | Remaining companies | Small, emerging companies      | High       | High       | Very High        | Patient investors with strong risk appetite |
⚠️ It’s pure `rank-based,` not a fixed rupee number.
---
##### Market Cap tells size, not quality  
Big ≠ good  
Small ≠ bad  

---  
Share price is low so it’s small cap. ❌  
Low price × many shares can still be large cap. ✔️
