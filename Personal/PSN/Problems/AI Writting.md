### Caption or Quotes
```txt
- overly balanced sentences (perfect symmetry like “this… that…”)
- generic emotions (love, destiny, forever — but nothing specific)
- no personal context (no “you”, no real situation, no memory)
- too polished language (sounds edited, not spoken)
- predictable patterns (contrast lines, philosophical tone)
- zero vulnerability (no doubt, fear, awkwardness)
- universal relatability (fits everyone → connects with no one)
- no sensory detail (no place, time, action, or environment)
- clean & complete thoughts (real humans leave things unfinished)
- cliché stacking (love + fate + destiny + world in 1 line)
- “quote format” writing (feels made to be posted, not said)
- emotionally safe (never risky, never controversial, never raw)
- repetition of structures (“Different X, same Y” again & again)
- lack of voice (no personality, could be written by anyone)
- no imperfections (no pauses, no broken grammar, no real rhythm)
```
