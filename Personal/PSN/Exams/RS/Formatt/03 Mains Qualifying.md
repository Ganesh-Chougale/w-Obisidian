# 2. Mains – 2A Qualifying Papers Format

## 2A1. Marathi

* **Type:** Descriptive (written)
* **Total Marks:** 100
* **Duration:** 3 hours
* **Qualifying Marks:** 35
* **Negative Marking:** ❌ No

### Paper structure:

* **Comprehension (आकलन)**

  * 1–2 passages + questions
  * ~20–30 marks

* **Precis Writing (संक्षेप लेखन)**

  * Summarize given passage
  * ~15–20 marks

* **Translation (भाषांतर)**

  * Marathi ↔ English
  * ~15–20 marks

* **Grammar & Language (व्याकरण)**

  * Sentence correction, vocabulary, usage
  * ~20–25 marks

* **Essay / Letter Writing (निबंध / पत्रलेखन)**

  * 1 topic
  * ~20–30 marks

---

## 2A2. English

* **Type:** Descriptive (written)
* **Total Marks:** 100
* **Duration:** 3 hours
* **Qualifying Marks:** 35
* **Negative Marking:** ❌ No

### Paper structure:

* **Comprehension**

  * Passage + questions
  * ~20–30 marks

* **Precis Writing**

  * Summary writing
  * ~15–20 marks

* **Translation**

  * English ↔ Marathi
  * ~15–20 marks

* **Grammar & Usage**

  * Error correction, vocabulary
  * ~20–25 marks

* **Essay / Letter Writing**

  * 1 topic
  * ~20–30 marks

---

## Important clarity

* No MCQs ❌
* Answers must be **written in full sentences/paragraphs**
* Marks are **section-based**
* Only need **35 marks to qualify**
* **Marks NOT added in final merit**

---

## Simple structure view

* Both papers = **basic language skill check**
* Focus = clarity, grammar, understanding (not high difficulty)
