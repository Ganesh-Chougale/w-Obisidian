## 17.1 Meaning of Hypothesis
`Hypothesis`: A testable statement or prediction about a population, situation, or relationship between variables.

- example: “Students who study more hours will score higher in exams.”
  * `Study Hours` → may affect → `Exam Score`

A hypothesis is not just a guess. It is a statement that can be tested using data.
`Research Question` → `Hypothesis` → `Collect Data` → `Test` → `Accept/Reject Evidence for Hypothesis`

### Remember
Hypothesis = Testable prediction.
