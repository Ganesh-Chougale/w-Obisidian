## 12.1 Primary Data  
`Primary data`: new/original data collected directly by the researcher for their own research question.  
- example: You want to study “The effect of contextual information on the quality of GEN-AI responses.” You give different prompts to GEN-AI, collect the responses, and analyse them yourself.   

Key idea: The researcher collects the data first-hand.  

`Researcher → Respondents/Source → New Data`  

Examples of collecting primary data:  

- Questionnaire/survey  
- Interview  
- Observation  
- Experiment  
- Direct measurement  

Easy memory:  
`Primary = I collected it myself.`  

Do not confuse: If you use data that someone else has already collected, it is secondary data.
