## 18.2 Difference between Means
`Difference between Means`: A statistical test used to determine whether the **means of two populations are significantly different**.  
- example: Compare the average exam scores of `Group A` and `Group B`.  
  * `Group A mean` = `72`  
  * `Group B mean` = `78`  
  * The test determines whether the `6-point difference` represents a **real population difference** or could have occurred due to sampling variation.  

`Mean A` − `Mean B` → `Difference` → `Statistical Test` → `Significant / Not Significant`  

### Remember
**Difference between means = “Are the two group averages really different?”**
