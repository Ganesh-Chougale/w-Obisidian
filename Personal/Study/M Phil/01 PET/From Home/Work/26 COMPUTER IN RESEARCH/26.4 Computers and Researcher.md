## 26.4 Computers and Researcher

`Computers and Researcher`: computers help researchers perform research tasks **faster, more accurately, and efficiently**.

- `Search` → find research papers and information.
- `Organize` → store and manage research data and references.
- `Analyze` → perform calculations and statistical analysis.
- `Write` → prepare and format research reports.
- `Present` → create charts, tables, and presentations.
- `Communicate` → share findings with others.

- example: A researcher studying `AI-assisted learning` can use a computer to collect survey responses, analyze the data, create graphs, and write the final report.

`Researcher` + `Computer` → `Search → Organize → Analyze → Write → Present` → `Efficient Research`
