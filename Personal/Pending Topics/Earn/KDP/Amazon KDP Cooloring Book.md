AI-Assisted Amazon KDP Coloring Book Business

1. BUSINESS MODEL
-----------------
Create coloring books and sell them through Amazon KDP.

KDP = Kindle Direct Publishing.

For physical paperback books:

Creator
   ↓
Creates book
   ↓
Uploads interior + cover to KDP
   ↓
Amazon lists the book
   ↓
Customer purchases
   ↓
Amazon prints the book
   ↓
Amazon ships the book
   ↓
Amazon handles customer service
   ↓
Amazon deducts printing costs/other applicable amounts
   ↓
Creator receives royalty


2. WHY THIS BUSINESS IS POSSIBLE
--------------------------------
Traditional publishing requires:
- Printing inventory
- Storage
- Packaging
- Shipping
- Upfront capital
- Publishers/distributors

KDP's print-on-demand model removes much of this infrastructure.

The creator does not need to manufacture and store hundreds of
physical books before knowing whether customers want them.

The physical book is produced when an order occurs.


3. WHAT YOU ACTUALLY SELL
-------------------------
You are not really selling "AI images."

You are selling a finished product.

Example:

"Bible Animals Coloring Book for Children Ages 4–8"

The product may contain:
- Cover
- 30 coloring pages
- Simple captions
- Appropriate illustrations
- Correct page dimensions
- Correct margins/bleed
- Proper PDF formatting
- Appropriate metadata
- Product description
- Search keywords
- Categories

- Printing problems

Submit the book for Amazon's review.

If approved:

Book becomes available for sale.

15. AI DISCLOSURE / COMPLIANCE
------------------------------
If AI actually generates the book's text, images, or translations,
KDP requires the creator to disclose that AI-generated content
when publishing.

AI-assisted content can be treated differently when the human
created the underlying content and AI only assisted with editing,
refining, brainstorming, etc.

Regardless:

The publisher remains responsible for the final content.


16. COPYRIGHT / INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
-------------------------------------
Do not assume:

"AI generated it"
=
"I can legally sell anything."

Before publishing, check:

- Copyright
- Trademarks
- Characters
- Logos
- Brand names
- Existing artwork
- Prompts/output rights under the AI tool's terms
- Amazon KDP content policies


Avoid creating products based on protected characters such as
Disney characters, unless you actually have the necessary rights.
