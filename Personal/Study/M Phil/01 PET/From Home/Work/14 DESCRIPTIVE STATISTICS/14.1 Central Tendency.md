## 14.1 Central Tendency
`Central Tendency`: A statistical measure that tells us the central or typical value of a dataset.  
The three main measures are:  
- `Mean` → Average of all values.  
- `Median` → Middle value when data is arranged in order.  
- `Mode` → Most frequently occurring value.  

- example: Data = `2, 3, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 8, 8, 9`  
  - `Mean` = 63 ÷ 11 = 5.73  
  - `Median` = 6 → the middle value  
  - `Mode` = 8 → occurs most often  

`Data` → `Mean / Median / Mode` → `Typical/Central Value`  

Remember:  
`Mean` = Average  
`Median` = Middle  
`Mode` = Most frequent  

Central tendency answers: “What value represents the centre of this data?”
