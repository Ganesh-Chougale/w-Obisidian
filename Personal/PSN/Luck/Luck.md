## `Luck` is perspective of Outcome.

Experiment No 1:
volunteer were gathered, & putted them on into stressful situations like mock job interviews, math solving problems (in simple words, stuff that increase cortisol[a stress hormones] level)
then 
gave them decision making task in limited time (Knapsack problems).
these problems are like cost & reward calculation but with quick decision
Eg: travel bag = how much weight & stuff to carry, what to remove & what to keep.
& taking these decision in time limit. (stress = time limit).

Findings of this experiment:
- decision accuracy decreased, wrong decision increased because of hurry
- the more complex problem more mistakes happened.
- people keep thinking negative more instead of positive & they rejected opportunities
- people starting avoid eye contacts while taking decision
- avoid spending time on important decision & want to move on fast

The mental fatigue:
mental fatigue make them do
- ruin simple decision
- most of people doesn't even reached at complex level

output was not so good result.


Experiment No 2:
A decision making game, 2 options will be given.
1 option gives reward, 1 option gives loss

this game played in 2 phases.
1. where people have no knowledge about game at all
2. where people has given explicit instructions which button gives reward & which button cause loss

so after phase 1 & before beginning phase 2 people know which option is good & which option is bad.

within this organisers categorized people in 3
A. Fast Learners: Players who did trial & error & did improvement in 1st phase
B. Correctors: Players who changed behaviour & choose safe option which is rewarding button in phase 2
C. Compulsive: Player who choose loss button even in phase 2

Most of time we do this compulsive player things
we know fast food is bad for health & yet we still consumes it.
we follow unhealthy habbits like scrolling instead of doing something productive
we choose bad but easy decision over good but difficult decision.

& When outcome comes instead of taking responsibility of own decision we blame it on our luck.


## so `Luck` do really exists or not?   
in simple words yes, luck do exists but its defination is not a boolean answer
i have 2 rational defination of luck: 
1. Absolute Luck 
- Things that we cannot controll or Random Events
eg.
    - where we born
    - a sudden accident
    - winning a lottery
    - unexpected opportunities
    etc.

2. Decisive luck 
- Random Events + the decision we made in past (here past could be 10mins ago, 10 days ago or even 10 years ago) + Current Mental State
eg.
Example A. you met your business partner in cafe
    A1. Randomness: you both were in same cafe
    A2. Past Decision: you decided to go cafe
    A3. Mental State: you were open minded & started conversation

Example B. Today is your job interview
    B1. Randomness: the person who is taking might be light hearted or might be strict
    B2. Past Decision: you decided to apply + your preparation
    B3. Mental State: are you stressed or calm

and as i said Luck is how we see at outcome.  
if everything was good we consider it as good luck, if not then we say its a bad luck
