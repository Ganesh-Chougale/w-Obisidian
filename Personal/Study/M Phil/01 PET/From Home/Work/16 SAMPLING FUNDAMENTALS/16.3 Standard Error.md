## 16.3 Standard Error
`Standard Error (SE)`: Measures how much a sample statistic, such as the sample mean, is expected to vary across different samples from the same population.  

- example: Take many samples of `30` people and calculate the mean height of each sample. The `Standard Error` tells us how much those sample means tend to differ from each other.  

`Repeated Samples` → `Different Sample Means` → `Standard Error = How Much They Vary`  

### Remember
- Standard Deviation → variation among individual data values.  
- Standard Error → variation among sample statistics (such as sample means).  

`SD` → variability of data  
`SE` → variability of sample statistic
