## 9.2 Steps in Sampling
`Sampling`: choosing a smaller group from a larger population to study.    
### Main steps
1. Define the population — Decide exactly who/what you want to study.  
- example: all university students using GEN-AI.  
2. Identify the sampling unit — Decide what one unit means.  
- example: one student.  
3. Choose the sample size — Decide how many units to study.  
- example: 500 students.  
4. Choose the sampling method — Decide how those 500 will be selected.  
- example: random sampling.  
5. Select the sample — Actually choose the participants.  
- example: randomly select 500 students.  
6. Collect data from the sample — Study the selected participants.  
- example: ask them how contextual information affects GEN-AI responses.  
Mental model:  
`Population → Sampling unit → Sample size → Sampling method → Select sample → Collect data`  
Exam yield: Remember the sequence, especially population → method → sample → data.
