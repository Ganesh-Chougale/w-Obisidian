## 18.8 Non-Parametric Tests
`Non-Parametric Tests`: Statistical tests that **do not require strong assumptions about the population distribution**, such as normality.  
Example: If data is ordinal, heavily skewed, or does not satisfy assumptions of a parametric test, a non-parametric test may be used.  
`Data` → `Distribution Assumptions Not Met` → `Non-Parametric Test` → `Statistical Decision`  

Common examples:  
* `Mann–Whitney U` → two independent groups  
* `Wilcoxon Signed-Rank` → paired data  
* `Kruskal–Wallis` → 3+ independent groups  

**Remember:** `Parametric` → stronger distribution assumptions; `Non-Parametric` → fewer distribution assumptions.
