## 17.4 Power of a Test

`Power of a Test`: The probability that a statistical test **correctly detects a real effect** when that effect actually exists.  
- example: A new teaching method really improves students' scores.  
  * A test with `80% power` has an `80% chance` of detecting that improvement.

`Real Effect Exists` → `Statistical Test` → `Correctly Detects It` = `Power`  

### Remember
`Power = 1 − β`  
where `β` = probability of **Type II error** (failing to detect a real effect).  

**High Power** → better chance of detecting a real effect.
