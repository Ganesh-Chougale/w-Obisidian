## 24.3 Types of Reports

`Types of Reports`: different forms of research reports prepared according to the **purpose, audience, and nature of the research**.

- `Technical Report` → detailed report for researchers or technical experts.
- `Popular Report` → simple, easy-to-read report for the general public.
- `Research Paper` → concise presentation of research for academic publication.
- `Thesis / Dissertation` → detailed research report submitted for an academic degree.
- `Project Report` → documents the work, findings, and outcomes of a specific project.

- example: The same research on `AI-assisted learning` could be written as a **technical report** for researchers or a **popular report** for teachers and students.

`Research` → `Purpose + Audience` → `Type of Report`
