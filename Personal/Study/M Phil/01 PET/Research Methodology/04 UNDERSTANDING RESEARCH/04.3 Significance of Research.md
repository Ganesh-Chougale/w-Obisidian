# 4.3 Significance of Research

## Core Idea

**Significance of research = Why research is valuable or useful.**

Research matters because it **creates knowledge, improves understanding, solves problems, supports decisions, and helps society progress.**

---

## Major Significance

### 1. Increases Knowledge

Research discovers new facts, relationships, principles, or explanations.

> Example: Investigating whether additional contextual information changes the quality of Gen-AI responses.

### 2. Solves Practical Problems

Applied research can provide solutions to real-world problems.

> Example: Developing guidelines for providing context to Gen-AI systems to improve response quality.

### 3. Helps in Decision-Making

Research provides evidence that can support better decisions instead of relying only on assumptions or opinions.

### 4. Develops and Tests Theories

Research can:

* develop new theories/concepts
* test existing theories
* modify or reject theories when evidence does not support them

### 5. Helps Predict Future Outcomes

When reliable relationships or patterns are identified, research can help make predictions.

### 6. Supports Planning and Policy

Governments, organizations, businesses, and institutions use research findings for planning, policy formulation, and evaluation.

### 7. Drives Innovation and Development

Research contributes to new technologies, methods, products, processes, and improvements in existing practices.

---

## Mental Model

**Research → Evidence → Knowledge → Understanding → Better Decisions / Solutions / Development**

This is the important chain.

---

## PET Memory

Remember the major purposes as:

**KNOW → UNDERSTAND → SOLVE → DECIDE → PREDICT → DEVELOP**

* **Know** new facts
* **Understand** phenomena
* **Solve** problems
* **Decide** using evidence
* **Predict** outcomes
* **Develop** theories, technology, policies, and practices

### One-second memory

> **Research converts uncertainty into evidence-based knowledge that can improve understanding, decisions, and action.**

### Don't confuse

**Significance ≠ Objective**

* **Objective** = what the research aims to accomplish.
* **Significance** = why the research is important/useful.
