## 18.3 Proportions
`Proportion`: The **fraction or percentage of a population having a particular characteristic**.  
`Test of Proportions`: A statistical test used to determine whether a population proportion **differs from a specified value or another population proportion**.  

- example: A company claims that `60%` of customers are satisfied.  
  * Survey `200` customers.  
  * `130` are satisfied → sample proportion = `65%`.  
  * Test whether the population satisfaction proportion is actually different from `60%`.  

### Common comparisons
- `One Proportion` → compare a sample proportion with a specified value.  
  * example: Is customer satisfaction different from `60%`?  

- `Two Proportions` → compare proportions from two groups.  
  * example: Is satisfaction different between `Group A` and `Group B`?  

`Sample Proportion(s)` → `Statistical Test` → `Compare` → `Decision`  

### Remember  
**Proportion = “What fraction/percentage has the characteristic?”**  
**Test of proportions = “Are the percentages really different?”**
