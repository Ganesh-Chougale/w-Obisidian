## 16.4 Estimation

`Estimation`: Using information from a **sample** to estimate an **unknown value (parameter)** of the population.  

- example: We want to know the average height of **all students** in a university, but cannot measure everyone.  

  * Take a sample of `100` students.  
  * Calculate their average height.  
  * Use that sample mean to **estimate** the population's average height.  

`Population` → `Sample` → `Calculate Statistic` → `Estimate Population Parameter`  

### Two main types

- `Point Estimation` → gives **one estimated value**.  
  * example: Estimated average height = `168 cm`  
- `Interval Estimation` → gives a **range of likely values**.  
  * example: Average height is estimated to be between `166–170 cm`.  

`Sample Information` → `Estimation` → `Unknown Population Parameter`
