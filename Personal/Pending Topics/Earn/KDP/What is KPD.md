================================================================================
                    AMAZON KDP BUSINESS FOR INDIVIDUALS
================================================================================

1. CORE CONCEPT
   • You act as an independent publisher, creating and uploading digital files
     (manuscripts and book covers) directly to Amazon's global marketplace.
   • The business operates on a Print-on-Demand (POD) model, meaning you hold 
     zero inventory, handle no shipping, and pay no upfront manufacturing fees.

2. WORKFLOW FOR AN INDIVIDUAL
   • Market Research: Find profitable niches on Amazon with high demand and low 
     competition (e.g., specific planners, romance tropes, or self-help topics).
   • Content Creation: Write the manuscript yourself, hire a ghostwriter, or 
     design interior layouts for low-content books using tools like Canva.
   • Cover Design: Create a highly clickable, professional book cover.
   • Formatting & Uploading: Convert files into KDP-ready formats, upload them, 
     set your pricing, and choose targeted keywords and categories.

3. MONETIZATION & COSTS
   • Royalties: Earn up to 70% on eBooks and up to 60% on physical books 
     (minus the baseline printing costs automatically deducted by Amazon).
   • Zero Startup Fee: Setting up an account and publishing is 100% free. 
   • Primary Costs: Your main capital investment will be for voluntary assets 
     like professional editing, cover design, or Amazon Advertising (Ads).

4. ADVANTAGES FOR INDIVIDUALS
   • True Passive Income: Once a book is uploaded, it can sell indefinitely 
     with minimal ongoing maintenance.
   • High Scalability: You can publish as many books as you want, targeting 
     different niches to build a diverse income portfolio.
   • Complete Control: You retain 100% ownership of your rights and intellectual 
     property, allowing you to change prices or change details at any time.

================================================================================
