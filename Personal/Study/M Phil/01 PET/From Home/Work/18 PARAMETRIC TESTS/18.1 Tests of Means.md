## 18.1 Tests of Means

`Tests of Means`: Statistical tests used to determine whether a **population mean differs from a specified value or another population mean**.  
- example: A company claims the average delivery time is `30 minutes`.  
  * Take a sample of deliveries.  
  * Calculate the sample mean.  
  * Test whether the actual population mean is significantly different from `30 minutes`.  

### Common types
- `One-Sample Test` → compares **one sample mean** with a specified value.  
  * example: Is the average delivery time different from `30 minutes`?  

- `Independent Two-Sample Test` → compares means of **two separate groups**.  
  * example: Is the average score of `Group A` different from `Group B`?  

- `Paired-Sample Test` → compares **two measurements from the same subjects**.  
  * example: Students' scores **before vs after** a training program.  

`Sample Mean(s)` → `Statistical Test` → `Compare with Expected/Other Mean` → `Decision`  

### Remember
**Tests of means = “Are these averages genuinely different, or could the difference be due to sampling variation?”**
