## 14.3 Skewness
`Skewness`: Measures how much a distribution is unevenly stretched to one side.  
Think of it as: “Is the data balanced around the centre, or does it have a long tail on one side?”  

- Symmetric → roughly balanced on both sides.  
- Positive / Right skewed → long tail toward higher values.  
- Negative / Left skewed → long tail toward lower values.  

- example:  
  `2, 3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 20`  

  Most values are small, but `20` stretches the distribution to the right → positive skewness.  

`Central Tendency` → Where is the centre?  
`Dispersion` → How spread out is it?  
`Skewness` → Which side is the data stretched toward?  

Remember: `Skewness = asymmetry of the distribution.`
