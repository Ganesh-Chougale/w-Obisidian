What does it take to be successful in life?
The definition of success differs from person to person.  
for some people a big house, a car, a yacht, a private plane.
for some people it can be money or net worth.  
for some people having relationships with the elite circle might count as success.  
some people can say a house full of people, a plate full of food and time to spend with loved ones counts as a success.   

so it's no universal optometry refraction for each pair of eyes.   

Success is completely personal.  
 
Someone values wealth.  
Someone values freedom.  
Someone values family.  
Someone values mastery.  
Someone values faith.  
Someone values influence.  

No one else can answer this for us.  

if we found what does success mean to us then we can follow this path. 
```
Personal Values 
        ▼
Definition of Success
        ▼
Choose a Direction
        ▼
Develop Skills
        ▼
Consistent Action
        ▼
Adapt to Reality
        ▼
Desired Outcome
```  

The first two boxes belong entirely to you.  
The remaining boxes belong mostly to reality.  
Reality doesn't care whether your dream is to own ten companies or to live quietly on a farm. It rewards competence, persistence, learning, and adaptation in both cases.
