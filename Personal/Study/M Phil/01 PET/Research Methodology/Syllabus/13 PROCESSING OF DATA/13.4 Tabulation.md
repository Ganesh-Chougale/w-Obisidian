## 13.4 Tabulation
`Tabulation`: Arranging classified data in **rows and columns** so it can be easily understood, compared, and analysed.  
* example:  
| GEN-AI Usage | No. of Respondents |
| ------------ | -----------------: |
| Daily        |                 40 |
| Weekly       |                 25 |
| Rarely       |                 20 |
| Never        |                 15 |

`Classified Data` → `Rows & Columns` → `Easy Comparison & Analysis`  

**Remember:**  
`Coding` = **give data a code**  
`Classification` = **group the data**    
`Tabulation` = **put those groups into a table**
