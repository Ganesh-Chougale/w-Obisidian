## 23.2 Why Interpretation?

`Interpretation`: helps connect the **research results to the research problem** and understand what the findings actually mean.

- It helps answer the **research question**.
- It connects findings with the **original problem or objectives**.
- It helps draw **meaningful conclusions**.

- example: Research problem → `Does AI-assisted study improve MCA students' exam performance?`
  - `Result` → AI-assisted students scored higher on average.
  - `Interpretation` → AI-assisted study may be associated with better exam performance.

`Statistical Results` → `Interpretation` → `Research Problem` → `Meaningful Conclusion`
