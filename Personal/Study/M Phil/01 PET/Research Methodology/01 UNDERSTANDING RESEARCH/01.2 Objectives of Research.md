# 1.2 Objectives of Research

## Core Idea

The **objective of research** is to systematically obtain knowledge that helps us **understand, describe, explain, predict, or solve** a problem.

### Major Objectives

### 1. Exploration

To gain **familiarity and new insights** about a problem or phenomenon.

Usually useful when the problem is not clearly understood.

**Example:**

> Exploring how people actually provide context to Gen-AI systems and what kinds of context they consider useful.

---

### 2. Description

To **accurately describe** the characteristics of a situation, group, phenomenon, or event.

**Example:**

> Describing the different types and amounts of context users provide to Gen-AI.

---

### 3. Explanation

To determine **why or how something happens**, often by examining relationships between variables.

**Example:**

> Investigating whether and why additional relevant context changes the quality of Gen-AI responses.

---

### 4. Prediction

To determine whether knowledge about one factor can help **predict another outcome**.

**Example:**

> Determining whether the amount/type of contextual information can predict the quality of an AI response.

---

### 5. Problem Solving / Application

To use research findings to **solve practical problems or improve existing practices**.

**Example:**

> Developing guidelines for providing context that helps users obtain better Gen-AI responses.

---

## Easy Mental Model

```text
Explore
   ↓
Describe
   ↓
Explain
   ↓
Predict
   ↓
Apply / Solve
```

These objectives are **not necessarily separate stages**. One research project may involve several of them.

## PET Memory

> **Research may aim to explore, describe, explain, predict, and solve/apply knowledge.**
