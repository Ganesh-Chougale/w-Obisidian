## 22.4 Factor Analysis

`Factor Analysis`: a technique used to **reduce many related observed variables into a smaller number of underlying factors**.

It looks for variables that are closely related and groups them under a common factor.

- example: A survey has questions about `camera quality, display, battery, performance, and speed`.  
  - `Performance + Speed` → **Performance Factor**
  - `Camera Quality + Display` → **Experience Factor**

`Many Observed Variables` → `Find Related Variables` → `Group into Factors` → `Fewer Underlying Factors`
