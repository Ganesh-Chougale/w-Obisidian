## 26.3 Computer Applications

`Computer Applications`: the different ways computers are used to **support various stages of the research process**.

- `Literature Review` → search, store, and organize research papers.
- `Data Collection` → create online surveys and record responses.
- `Data Analysis` → perform calculations and statistical analysis.
- `Report Writing` → write and format the research report.
- `Presentation` → create charts, slides, and visualizations.
- `Communication` → share research findings electronically.

- example: A researcher uses a computer to `find papers → collect survey data → analyze results → write the report → present findings`.
