# 2.4 Conceptual vs Empirical Research

## Core Difference

The simplest distinction:

> **Conceptual research → Ideas, concepts, theories, and models.**
> **Empirical research → Observation, experiment, and actual evidence/data.**

---

# 1. Conceptual Research

### Meaning

**Conceptual research is concerned with developing, examining, or clarifying concepts, theories, principles, or ideas.**

It does not necessarily require collecting new observations or experimental data.

It mainly asks:

> **"What should we think about this concept, and how can we explain it?"**

### Our Example

Research topic:

> **The effect of contextual information on the quality of GEN-AI responses.**

A conceptual study might develop a model explaining how contextual information could influence AI response quality:

```text
Context
   ↓
Better understanding of user intent
   ↓
More relevant response
   ↓
Higher response quality
```

The researcher is developing or examining the **conceptual relationship** between context and response quality.

---

# 2. Empirical Research

### Meaning

**Empirical research is based on observation or experiment and uses actual evidence/data to investigate a question.**

It mainly asks:

> **"What does the actual evidence show?"**

### Our Example

We could conduct an experiment:

```text
Same Task
   ↓
Different Context Levels
   ↓
Generate AI Responses
   ↓
Measure Response Quality
   ↓
Analyse Results
```

For example:

| Context                   | Quality Score |
| ------------------------- | ------------: |
| No context                |          4/10 |
| Basic context             |          7/10 |
| Detailed relevant context |          8/10 |

Those observations and measurements provide **empirical evidence**.

---

# Key Difference

| Conceptual                           | Empirical                            |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Ideas and concepts                   | Actual observations/data             |
| Develops or examines theories/models | Tests/examines using evidence        |
| May not collect new data             | Relies on observed/measured evidence |
| "What might explain this?"           | "What does the evidence show?"       |
| Theory/model oriented                | Observation/experiment oriented      |

---

# Same Research Topic — Two Approaches

### Conceptual

> **How might contextual information influence the quality of Gen-AI responses?**

→ Develop a theoretical/conceptual model.

### Empirical

> **Does changing contextual information actually change Gen-AI response quality?**

→ Collect responses and analyse actual evidence.

---

# Important Relationship

Conceptual and empirical research can **work together**.

```text
CONCEPTUAL
Idea / Theory / Model
        ↓
     Hypothesis
        ↓
EMPIRICAL
Collect Evidence
        ↓
     Test / Analyse
        ↓
Support / Modify / Reject the idea
```

For example, we might conceptually propose:

> **Relevant context improves response quality.**

Then empirically test whether the evidence supports that proposition.
