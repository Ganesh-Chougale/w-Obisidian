## 1.1 Meaning of Research
- a systematic process of finding answers or generating new knowledge, supported by solid evidence.
```
- research is not just a trial & error & find out what works better
- its about why it worked & how it worked with solid evidences
```
it involves
```txt
1. Explore → investigate something not sufficiently understood
2. Describe → understand/document what exists
3. Clarify → remove ambiguity or improve understanding
4. Estimate → determine approximate values/relationships
5. Solve the problem → address a specific practical or theoretical problem
```
