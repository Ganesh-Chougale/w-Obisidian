# Hair Cycle

This explains WHY hair naturally falls.

Each hair follows a cycle independently.

---

# Main Hair Cycle Stages

## 1. Anagen Phase (Growth Phase)

This is the active growth stage.

Hair grows continuously.

Can last:
2–7 years.

Longer anagen:
→ longer hair potential

---

## 2. Catagen Phase (Transition Phase)

Small temporary phase.

Follicle slows down activity.

Usually:
few weeks.

---

## 3. Telogen Phase (Resting Phase)

Hair rests.

No active growth.

After resting:
hair gets released.

---

## 4. Exogen Phase (Shedding)

Hair falls out.

Then new hair starts growing from same follicle.

Cycle repeats.

---

# Why This Matters

If too many hairs enter:
telogen/shedding phase together,

you notice:

* more hair in comb
* more hair during shampoo

This can happen after:

* stress
* illness
* dandruff
* poor diet
* sleep issues

---

# Simple Analogy

Imagine scalp as a farm.

## Follicles

= soil spots

## Hair strands

= crops

### Shedding

Old crop naturally falling.

### Miniaturization

Soil becoming weaker and producing smaller crops every season.

That’s the dangerous part.
