# `Current Price`
The price you pay right now for one share.  
That’s it. No hidden meaning.  

#### What “Current Price” actually represents
It is the last price at which a buyer and seller agreed  
Not:  
- company’s true value
- company’s profit
- company’s future
Just the latest agreed deal price.  

`A live negotiation, not a fixed tag.`  
Price changes because people change their bids and offers — not because the company changes every minute.  

### Behind the scene of current price  
Current Price = the price at which the last buyer and seller agreed to trade.  
- Buyer: “I’m willing to pay ₹X”
- Seller: “I’ll sell for ₹Y”
- Deal happens only if `X ≥ Y`
- That agreed price becomes the Current Price  
No company announcement, no profit update — just a mutual decision between market participants. `A live auction`  
#### At every second, the exchange has two lists:  
- Buyers → “I will buy at ₹X”
- Sellers → “I will sell at ₹Y”
No deal happens until X = Y.  
📌 That matched price = Current Price  
##### Example:
Assume current price is ₹100  
1. `Agreement`: if all buyers & sellers agree on any of these price, that becomes current price.  
- `case 1`: More buyers comes, if one buyer has purchasing power more than `101` & decides to buy a share at `101`, New Current Price = `₹101`. single share price hiked by `₹1`. 
- `case 2`:  More Sellers comes, if one seller decides to sell at `99`, New Current Price = `₹99`. single share price dropped by `₹1`. almost everytime price dropping caused by seller panic.     
2. `Idle`: if no agreement, price stays `₹100`  

##### Resting order price: 
- Seller wants ₹100
- Buyer offers ₹101
> Trade executes at ₹100, not ₹101 (because seller was willing to sell at 100 [the order price]).  
> This depends on order type, but generally the resting order price is used.
