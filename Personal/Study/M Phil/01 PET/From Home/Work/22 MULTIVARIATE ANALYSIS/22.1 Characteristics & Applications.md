## 22.1 Characteristics & Applications

`Multivariate Analysis`: a statistical method that studies **multiple variables at the same time** to understand their relationships and combined effects.

### Characteristics
- Studies **several variables simultaneously**.
- Examines relationships between variables.
- Helps understand **complex real-world situations**.
- Often used to identify which variables have the greatest influence on an outcome.

### Applications
- `Marketing` → study how price, advertising, and product quality affect sales.
- `Education` → study how attendance, study time, and previous marks affect exam performance.
- `Healthcare` → study how age, lifestyle, diet, and exercise relate to health outcomes.

- example: To find what affects students' exam scores, a researcher studies `study time + attendance + sleep + previous marks` together.

`Multiple Variables` → `Analyze Together` → `Relationships / Effects` → `Better Understanding`
