## 9.4 Non-Probability Sampling
`Non-Probability Sampling`: population units are selected without random selection, so their exact chance of being selected is unknown.
- example: asking 100 students who are easily available to participate in a study.

### Main types
`Convenience Sampling`: select whoever is easiest to reach or available.
- example: survey students available in your classroom.
`Judgment / Purposive Sampling`: researcher deliberately selects people who fit the study.
- example: selecting experienced GEN-AI users for a study.
`Quota Sampling`: divide people into groups and collect a fixed number from each group, but selection within groups is not random.
- example: 50 Engineering + 50 Arts students chosen conveniently.
`Snowball Sampling`: existing participants refer other participants.
- example: one GEN-AI researcher introduces you to other GEN-AI researchers.
Mental model:
`Non-Probability = Non-random selection + Unknown chance`
Exam yield: Know the 4 types + how participants are selected.
