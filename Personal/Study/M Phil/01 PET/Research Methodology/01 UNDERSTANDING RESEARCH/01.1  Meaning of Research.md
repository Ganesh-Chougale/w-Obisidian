# 1.1 Meaning of Research

## Core Idea

**Research is a systematic investigation undertaken to answer a question, solve a problem, or discover/establish knowledge using evidence.**

### Mental Model

```text
UNKNOWN / PROBLEM
        ↓
     QUESTION
        ↓
SYSTEMATIC INVESTIGATION
        ↓
     EVIDENCE
        ↓
     ANALYSIS
        ↓
KNOWLEDGE / CONCLUSION
```

## What Makes Something Research?

Research generally involves:

1. **A question or problem** — something that needs to be understood or investigated.
2. **Systematic investigation** — following a planned and logical process rather than casually looking for information.
3. **Evidence** — collecting relevant observations, measurements, data, or existing information.
4. **Analysis** — examining the evidence to identify patterns, relationships, differences, or explanations.
5. **Conclusion** — reaching an evidence-based answer or adding to existing knowledge.

## Research ≠ Information Gathering

Reading a book, searching the internet, or asking questions can provide information, but they are not automatically research.

**Research = systematic investigation + evidence + analysis + conclusion.**

## Running Example

**Research topic:**

> *The effect of contextual information on the quality of GEN-AI responses.*

A research question could be:

> *How does providing different amounts or types of contextual information affect the quality of GEN-AI responses?*

Simply believing that context improves AI responses is **not research**.

Systematically testing different contextual conditions, collecting responses, evaluating them, analysing the results, and drawing an evidence-based conclusion **is research**.

## One-Line Memory

> **Research is a systematic investigation that uses evidence to answer questions, solve problems, or establish knowledge.**
