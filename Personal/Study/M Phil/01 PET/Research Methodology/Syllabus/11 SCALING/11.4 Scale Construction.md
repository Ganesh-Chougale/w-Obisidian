## 11.4 Scale Construction
`Scale construction`: creating a set of questions/statements and assigning values to measure a characteristic in a consistent way.  

- example: To measure satisfaction with GEN-AI, create statements such as “GEN-AI gives useful answers” and let users respond from `1 = Strongly disagree` to `5 = Strongly agree`.  

### Basic process

`Define what to measure` → `Create items` → `Assign scale values` → `Test the scale` → `Finalise scale`  

- Define: Decide exactly what characteristic you want to measure.  
- Create items: Prepare questions/statements that represent that characteristic.  
- Assign values: Decide how responses will be scored.  
- Test: Check whether the scale gives consistent and meaningful results.  
- Finalise: Keep the useful items and remove/improve weak ones.  

Mental model:  
`Characteristic → Questions/Statements → Values → Test → Usable Scale`  

we have to be topic snipper setting a questions/statement precisely according to topic. narrowing down broader concept to precise target.
