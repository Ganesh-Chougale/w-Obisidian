## 26.1 Computer System

`Computer System`: a combination of **hardware and software** used to collect, store, process, analyze, and present research information.

- `Hardware` → physical components such as `computer, keyboard, storage, printer`.
- `Software` → programs used for tasks such as `data analysis, word processing, and presentation`.

- example: A researcher uses a `computer + spreadsheet/statistical software` to enter survey data, analyze it, and prepare the research report.

`Hardware + Software` → `Process Research Data` → `Information / Results`
