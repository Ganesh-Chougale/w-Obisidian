## 5.4 Technique of Defining the Problem
The techniques used to turn a **broad topic into a clear, specific, and researchable problem**.
* **Identify the broad area** → What general topic are you interested in?
* **Find the specific issue** → What exactly is unclear or problematic?
* **Review existing knowledge** → What is already known about it?
* **Identify the gap** → What is still unknown or needs further investigation?
* **Set boundaries** → Who/what, where, and when will be studied?
* **State the problem clearly** → Express it as a specific research problem or question.

* example:
  **Broad topic:** AI in education
  → **Issue:** Students are increasingly using AI for studying, but its effect on their learning is uncertain.
  → **Gap:** Its effect on exam performance is unclear
  → **Boundary:** MCA students in one college
  → **Research problem:** *What is the effect of AI-assisted study on the exam performance of MCA students?*

`Defining a Problem` → **Broad Topic → Specific Issue → Gap → Boundaries → Clear Problem**
