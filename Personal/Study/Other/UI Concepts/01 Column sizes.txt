Default (xs / Extra Small): For extra-small screens, you don't use a prefix. You simply use col-6 to span half the width on the smallest screens and up.

Small (sm): col-sm-6 means columns stack vertically on extra-small screens, but become horizontal (50% width) on small screens (576px) and larger.

Medium (md): col-md-4 means columns become 33.3% width only on medium screens (768px) and larger.
