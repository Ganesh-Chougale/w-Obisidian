# 3. Mains – Merit Papers Format

Conducted by Union Public Service Commission

## 3A. Essay Paper

* **Type:** Descriptive
* **Total Marks:** 250
* **Duration:** 3 hours
* **Negative Marking:** ❌ No

### Paper structure:

* Usually **2 essays to be written**
* Essay sections may include topics from:

  * Social issues
  * Economy
  * Politics
  * International affairs
  * Ethics / philosophy
  * Science & technology
  * Governance

### Marks depend on:

* Structure (intro–body–conclusion)
* Clarity of thought
* Balance & multidimensional analysis
* Examples & flow
* Language quality

---

## Important clarity

* All papers are written/descriptive (no MCQs)
* Answers must be structured properly

  * Introduction
  * Body
  * Conclusion
* Word limits are strict
* Time management is extremely important
* Marks ARE counted in final merit

---

## Simple structure view

* Essay → Expression + analysis
* GS1 → History + society + geography
* GS2 → Polity + governance + IR
* GS3 → Economy + security + science
* GS4 → Ethics + case studies
* Optional → Deep specialization subject
