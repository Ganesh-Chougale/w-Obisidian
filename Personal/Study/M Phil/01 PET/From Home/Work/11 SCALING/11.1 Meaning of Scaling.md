## 11.1 Meaning of Scaling
`Scaling`: Scaling is more specifically the process of constructing/using a scale to measure the degree of a characteristic.  
- example: measuring how satisfied students are with GEN-AI using `1 = Very dissatisfied` to `5 = Very satisfied`.  
Mental model:  
`Characteristic → Degree/Intensity → Scale`  
Exam yield:  
`Measurement` → assigns a value.  
`Scaling` → shows how much / how strongly a characteristic exists.
