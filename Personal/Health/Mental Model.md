> I used to go for running every morning, but it always felt like torture to wake up early, run, and then get tired. I used to think, **"Why am I exhausting myself on purpose just to stay fit?"** It felt like too much effort.
>
> Then one day, I randomly watched a YouTube video explaining how fat actually leaves our body. The video went into the science behind it. I don't remember all the details now, but I remember one key thing: when we exercise and burn fat, a significant part of it eventually leaves the body through the carbon dioxide we exhale.
>
> That completely changed how I looked at running.
>
> Earlier, when I was exhausted and breathing heavily, I only saw it as **fatigue**. But after understanding what was happening inside my body, I started seeing every heavy breath differently.
>
> **Every exhausting exhale now felt rewarding.**
>
> I knew that the exhaustion wasn't just pointless suffering. My body was actually doing something. That small change in how I understood the process made me much more consistent with my workouts. After that, there was barely a day when I skipped my workout.
