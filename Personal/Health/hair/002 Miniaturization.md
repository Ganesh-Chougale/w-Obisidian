# Miniaturization

This is one of the MOST important concepts.

Miniaturization means:

> thick healthy hair slowly becomes thinner and weaker over time

Example:

### Stage 1

Healthy hair:

* thick
* dark
* strong

↓

### Stage 2

Hair becomes:

* thinner
* softer
* smaller

↓

### Stage 3

Very tiny hair:

* weak
* almost invisible

↓

### Stage 4

Follicle becomes inactive

---

This usually happens in:
Androgenetic Alopecia

And mainly due to DHT sensitivity.

---

## Important Difference

### Shedding

= hair falls

### Miniaturization

= follicle becomes weaker over years

This is why:
hair fall alone is NOT the real enemy.

Follicle weakening is more serious.
