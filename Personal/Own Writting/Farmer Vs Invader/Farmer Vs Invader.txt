Farmer Vs invader mentality.

Farmer:
Believes in long-term growth
Designs, builds, and cultivates
Creates value through effort and patience
Contributes to society


Invader:
Seeks quick gain
Takes from others instead of creating
Avoids the effort required to build something
Focuses on short-term advantage

Warrior (Ideal):
A creator at core (like a Farmer)
Protects what is built when required
Balances growth with strength
Uses force only when necessary, not as a default
Ensures safety of people, values, and creation
