# 1.3 Motivation in Research

## Core Idea

**Research motivation is the reason or driving force that makes a researcher undertake a research study.**

In simple terms:

> **Why does the researcher want to investigate this problem?**

---

## Common Sources of Research Motivation

### 1. Curiosity / Desire for Knowledge

The researcher wants to understand something that is unknown or insufficiently understood.

**Example:**

> Why does adding certain contextual information improve some Gen-AI responses but not others?

---

### 2. Solving a Practical Problem

The researcher wants to solve a real-world problem.

**Example:**

> Users often provide poor context and receive poor AI responses. Research may help identify better ways to provide context.

---

### 3. Intellectual Challenge

The researcher wants to investigate an interesting or difficult question and develop deeper understanding.

---

### 4. Previous Research / Research Gap

Existing studies may leave unanswered questions, contradictions, limitations, or unexplored areas.

**Example:**

> Previous research may show that context matters, but leave unclear which types of context have the greatest effect on response quality.

This can motivate a new study.

---

### 5. Social or Practical Importance

Research may be motivated by the potential benefit to society, organisations, education, industry, policy, etc.

---

### 6. Personal / Professional Motivation

A researcher may be motivated by academic interest, professional development, career goals, or a personal interest in the problem.

---

## Important Distinction

**Objective ≠ Motivation**

|                | Meaning                                |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Objective**  | What the research aims to achieve      |
| **Motivation** | Why the researcher wants to conduct it |

Example:

> **Motivation:** AI users often receive inconsistent responses when they provide different amounts of context.

> **Objective:** To investigate how contextual information affects the quality of Gen-AI responses.

### Mental Model

```text
WHY?
↓
Motivation
↓
WHAT DO I WANT TO ACHIEVE?
↓
Objective
↓

```
