## 24.2 Layout of Research Report

`Layout of Research Report`: the **standard arrangement of sections** used to present a research study in a clear and logical order.

A typical research report includes:

- `Title` → name of the research.
- `Introduction` → background, problem, objectives, and scope.
- `Literature Review` → previous research related to the topic.
- `Methodology` → methods used to conduct the research.
- `Results / Findings` → what the research discovered.
- `Discussion / Interpretation` → what the findings mean.
- `Conclusion` → main conclusions drawn from the study.
- `References` → sources used in the research.
- `Appendices` → supporting material, if required.

`Title` → `Introduction` → `Literature Review` → `Methodology` → `Results` → `Discussion` → `Conclusion` → `References` → `Appendices`
