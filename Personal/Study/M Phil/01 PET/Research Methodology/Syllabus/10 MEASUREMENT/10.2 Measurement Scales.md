## 10.2 Measurement Scales
`Measurement Scales`: tell us what the numbers mean and what comparisons/calculations we can make with them.  
### 4 Main Scales
`Nominal`: numbers are just labels/names; no order.  
- example: Gender → `1 = Male, 2 = Female`  
`Ordinal`: values have an order/rank, but the gap between them is not necessarily equal.  
- example: Satisfaction → `1 = Low, 2 = Medium, 3 = High`  
`Interval`: values have equal gaps, but no true zero.  
- example: Temperature → `10°C, 20°C, 30°C`  
`Ratio`: equal gaps and a true zero; all normal arithmetic comparisons are meaningful.  
- example: 20 kg is twice 10 kg
### Easy memory
`Nominal → Order → Equal Interval → True Zero`  
Exam yield:
`Nominal = Name`  
`Ordinal = Rank`  
`Interval = Equal gap, no true zero`  
`Ratio = Equal gap + true zero`
