# Who decides **Dividend**

```text
Company's Board of Directors decides the dividend
```

But it is usually **approved by shareholders**.

---

# Step-by-step process

### 1️⃣ Company earns profit

Example:

| Item         | Value       |
| ------------ | ----------- |
| Total Profit | ₹1000 crore |

Management decides what to do with it.

---

### 2️⃣ Board of Directors proposes dividend

The **Board of Directors** (company leadership) decides:

```text
Dividend = ₹5 per share
```

They consider things like:

* future expansion
* debt
* cash reserves
* stability of business

---

### 3️⃣ Shareholders approve it

In the **Annual General Meeting (AGM)**:

Shareholders vote to approve the dividend proposal.

Normally they agree because:

```text
Board recommendation already reflects company finances
```

---

# Who are the Board of Directors?

They are people responsible for running and supervising the company.

Examples of board members may include:

* CEO / Managing Director
* independent directors
* industry experts

Their job is to act in **shareholders’ interest**.

---

# Example

If a company has:

| Shares   | 50 crore     |
| -------- | ------------ |
| Dividend | ₹4 per share |

Total payout:

```text
50 crore × ₹4 = ₹200 crore
```

The board must ensure the company can **afford this payout safely**.

---

# Important rule (India)

A company **cannot pay dividend if it has no profit**
(except in rare cases using past reserves).

This rule is governed by the **Securities and Exchange Board of India** and the **Companies Act 2013**.

---

# One line summary

```text
Board of Directors decides the dividend,
Shareholders approve it.
```
