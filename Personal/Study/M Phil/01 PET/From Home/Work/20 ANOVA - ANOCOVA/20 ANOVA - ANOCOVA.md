## 20 ANOVA / ANCOVA
`ANOVA (Analysis of Variance)`: Tests whether the **means of 3 or more groups** are significantly different.
Example: Compare average exam scores of students using 3 different teaching methods.  
`Group Means` → `ANOVA` → `Significant Difference / No Significant Difference`  
`ANCOVA (Analysis of Covariance)`: Compares group means **while statistically controlling for another variable (covariate)** that may affect the outcome.  
Example: Compare teaching methods while controlling for students' **previous exam scores**.  
`Group Means` + `Control Variable` → `ANCOVA` → `Adjusted Mean Comparison`  

**Remember:**  
* `ANOVA` → **Compare means**  
* `ANCOVA` → **Compare means + control another variable**
