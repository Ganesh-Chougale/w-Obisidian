## 15.4 Partial Correlation
`Partial Correlation`: Measures the relationship between two variables while keeping the effect of another variable(s) constant.  

- example:
  * `Exercise (X)` ↔ `Weight (Y)`  
  * `Diet (Z)` → controlled/held constant  

This asks: “What is the relationship between Exercise and Weight after removing the effect of Diet?”  
`X ↔ Y` → control Z → `remaining relationship`  

### Mental model  
> “What is the relationship between X and Y when another factor is controlled?”  
Remember:  
`Correlation` → relationship between `X` and `Y`  
`Partial Correlation` → relationship between `X` and `Y` after controlling other variables
