## 22.7 Path Analysis

`Path Analysis`: a technique used to study **how variables are directly or indirectly related to each other**.

It helps show the possible **path of influence** from one variable to another.

- example:
  - `Study Time` → `Knowledge` → `Exam Score`
  - `Study Time` → `Exam Score`
  
  Here, study time may have a **direct effect** on exam score and an **indirect effect** through knowledge.

`Cause/Factor` → `Direct / Indirect Path` → `Outcome`
