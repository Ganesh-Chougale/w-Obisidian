## 15.2 Multiple Correlation

`Multiple Correlation`: Measures the combined relationship of two or more independent variables (`X₁, X₂, ...`) with one dependent variable (`Y`).  

- example:

  * `Study Hours (X₁)`  
  * `Attendance (X₂)`  
  * `Exam Marks (Y)`  

It examines how strongly Study Hours + Attendance together are related to Exam Marks.  
`X₁ + X₂ + X₃ ...` → Multiple Correlation → `Y`  
### Mental model  
> “How strongly are several X factors together related to Y?”  
Remember: Multiple correlation ≠ proving that the X variables *cause* Y.
