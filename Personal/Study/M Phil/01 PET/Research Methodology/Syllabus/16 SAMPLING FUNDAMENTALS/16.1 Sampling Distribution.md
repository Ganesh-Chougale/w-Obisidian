## 16.1 Sampling Distributions  
`Sampling Distribution`: The distribution of a statistic (such as mean or proportion) obtained by taking many repeated samples from the same population.  

- example:
  * Take many samples of `100 students`.
  * Calculate the `mean` marks for each sample.
  * The collection of those sample means forms a sampling distribution.

`Population` → `Many Samples` → `Statistic from each sample` → `Sampling Distribution`  

### Mental model
> “If I keep taking samples, how does my calculated statistic vary?”
