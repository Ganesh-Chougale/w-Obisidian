# Shedding (Hair Shedding)
“Hair shedding” simply means:
> normal or increased falling of hair strands from scalp
Every human sheds hair daily.
Because hair is NOT permanent.
Each hair has its own lifespan.
So:
* hair grows
* rests
* falls
* new hair replaces it
That falling process = shedding.
---
## Normal Shedding
Usually:
* during shampoo
* combing
* pillow
* towel
You may lose:
around 50–100 hairs/day normally.
That alone is NOT baldness.
---
## Excessive Shedding
Problem happens when:
* too many hairs fall
* hair density reduces
* shedding continues for months
Common reasons:
* dandruff/inflammation
* stress
* illness
* fever
* poor nutrition
* sudden weight loss
* androgenetic alopecia
---
```

# Miniaturization
This is one of the MOST important concepts.
Miniaturization means:
> thick healthy hair slowly becomes thinner and weaker over time
Example:
### Stage 1
Healthy hair:
* thick
* dark
* strong
↓
### Stage 2
Hair becomes:
* thinner
* softer
* smaller
↓
### Stage 3
Very tiny hair:
* weak
* almost invisible
↓
### Stage 4
Follicle becomes inactive
---
This usually happens in:
Androgenetic Alopecia
And mainly due to DHT sensitivity.
---
## Important Difference
### Shedding
= hair falls
### Miniaturization
= follicle becomes weaker over years
This is why:
hair fall alone is NOT the real enemy.
Follicle weakening is more serious.
---
```

# Hair Structure
Hair is not “just one thing”.
It has parts.
---
# Simple Hair Structure
## A. Hair Shaft
This is the visible part outside scalp.
The part you comb.
It is basically dead protein.
Mainly keratin.
---
## B. Hair Root
The lower part inside skin.
Connected to follicle.
---
## C. Hair Follicle
Very important.
Tiny organ-like structure inside scalp that produces hair.
Think of follicle like:
a factory that manufactures hair strands.
Healthy follicle:
→ thick hair
Weak follicle:
→ thin hair
Dead follicle:
→ no visible hair
---
## D. Sebaceous Gland
Produces scalp oil (sebum).
Too much oil + fungus + inflammation:
can contribute to dandruff.
---
## E. Blood Supply
Follicles need:
* oxygen
* protein
* minerals
* nutrients
Poor health affects this system.
---
```

# Hair Cycle
This explains WHY hair naturally falls.
Each hair follows a cycle independently.
---
# Main Hair Cycle Stages
## 1. Anagen Phase (Growth Phase)
This is the active growth stage.
Hair grows continuously.
Can last:
2–7 years.
Longer anagen:
→ longer hair potential
---
## 2. Catagen Phase (Transition Phase)
Small temporary phase.
Follicle slows down activity.
Usually:
few weeks.
---
## 3. Telogen Phase (Resting Phase)
Hair rests.
No active growth.
After resting:
hair gets released.
---
## 4. Exogen Phase (Shedding)
Hair falls out.
Then new hair starts growing from same follicle.
Cycle repeats.
---
# Why This Matters
If too many hairs enter:
telogen/shedding phase together,
you notice:
* more hair in comb
* more hair during shampoo
This can happen after:
* stress
* illness
* dandruff
* poor diet
* sleep issues
---
# Simple Analogy
Imagine scalp as a farm.
## Follicles
= soil spots
## Hair strands
= crops
### Shedding
Old crop naturally falling.
### Miniaturization
Soil becoming weaker and producing smaller crops every season.
That’s the dangerous part.
---
```
