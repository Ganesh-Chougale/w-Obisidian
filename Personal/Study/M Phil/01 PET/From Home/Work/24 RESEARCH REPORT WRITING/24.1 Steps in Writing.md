## 24.1 Steps in Writing

`Steps in Writing`: the sequence followed to **organise and present research findings clearly and logically**.

- `Plan` → decide what to include and how to organise it.
- `Organise` → arrange findings in a logical order.
- `Draft` → write the first version.
- `Review` → check accuracy, clarity, and completeness.
- `Edit` → correct language, structure, and presentation.
- `Finalise` → prepare the final research report.

`Plan` → `Organise` → `Draft` → `Review` → `Edit` → `Finalise`
