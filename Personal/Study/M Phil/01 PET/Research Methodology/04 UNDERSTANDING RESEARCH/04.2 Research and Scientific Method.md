# 4.2 Research and Scientific Method

## Core Idea

**Scientific method is a systematic way of investigating questions using observation, evidence, testing, and logical reasoning.**

Research often uses the scientific method to produce **reliable and objective knowledge**.

### Mental Model

```text
Observation
    ↓
Question / Problem
    ↓
Hypothesis
    ↓
Test / Collect Evidence
    ↓
Analyse
    ↓
Conclusion
    ↓
Revise / Further Investigation
```

---

# Scientific Method — Key Features

### 1. Observation

The researcher observes a phenomenon or identifies a problem.

**Our example:**

> Gen-AI responses appear to improve when more relevant context is provided.

---

### 2. Formulate a Problem / Question

Turn the observation into a researchable question.

> **Does contextual information affect the quality of Gen-AI responses?**

---

### 3. Formulate a Hypothesis

Make a **testable proposition** about what you expect to happen.

> **Providing relevant contextual information improves Gen-AI response quality.**

---

### 4. Collect Evidence / Experiment

Systematically collect data that can test the hypothesis.

For example:

> Give the same task to Gen-AI under different contextual conditions and record the responses.

---

### 5. Analyse

Examine the collected evidence using appropriate methods.

For example:

> Compare response-quality scores between different context conditions.

---

### 6. Draw a Conclusion

Determine what the evidence indicates regarding the hypothesis.

The evidence may:

* Support the hypothesis.
* Fail to support the hypothesis.
* Lead to a revised explanation.

---

# Important Characteristics of the Scientific Method

### Systematic

Research follows an organised process rather than random investigation.

### Empirical

Conclusions are based on **observable or measurable evidence**.

### Objective

The researcher attempts to minimise personal bias and let evidence guide the conclusion.

### Testable

Claims should be capable of being examined using evidence.

### Replicable

The procedure should be described clearly enough that other researchers can repeat the investigation.

### Self-Correcting

Scientific knowledge can be modified when better evidence becomes available.

---

# Research vs Scientific Method

These are related but **not identical**.

> **Research = the broader systematic investigation undertaken to answer a question or solve a problem.**

> **Scientific method = a systematic way of conducting investigation using observation, hypothesis, testing, evidence, and reasoning.**

Research may use the scientific method, particularly in scientific and empirical studies.

---

# Running Example

```text
Observation
"AI responses seem better with relevant context."
        ↓
Question
"Does context affect response quality?"
        ↓
Hypothesis
"Relevant context improves response quality."
        ↓
Test
"Change context while keeping the task constant."
        ↓
Data
"Record response-quality scores."
        ↓
Analysis
"Compare the results."
        ↓
Conclusion
"What does the evidence support?"
```
