# Early Seeding

Early seeding begins with identifying a field, technology, idea, or shift early, understanding where it may be heading, and taking meaningful action before the field becomes crowded.

## Process

**1. Discover Early**  
Find a field, technology, idea, or shift that is still relatively small or underdeveloped.

↓

**2. Understand Early**  
Learn not just *what it is*, but:

- Why is it emerging?
- What problem does it solve?
- What could make it grow?
- What needs to happen for adoption?

↓

**3. Act Early**  
Start building knowledge, skills, projects, relationships, or products around it.

↓

**4. Validate the Direction**

Ask:

> **Am I early to something valuable, or merely early to something that won't take off?**

## The Important Distinction

**Early ≠ Automatically Advantageous**

| Position | What happens |
|---|---|
| Too late | The field is already crowded |
| **Early + right** | **You have time to build an advantage** |
| Early + wrong | You spend years on something that never becomes significant |

## A Better Mindset

You don't need to predict the future perfectly.

You need to recognize **signals** early enough to investigate them, while keeping your commitment proportional to the evidence.

Instead of:

> "I know this will become big."

Think:

> **"I see enough signals that this might become important, so I'll start learning and experimenting before the majority notices it."**

## Core Idea

**Discover → Understand → Experiment → Validate → Commit**

The real skill is not simply **being early**.

It is learning to recognize **when being early is an advantage** and **when you are simply early to something that will never take off.**
