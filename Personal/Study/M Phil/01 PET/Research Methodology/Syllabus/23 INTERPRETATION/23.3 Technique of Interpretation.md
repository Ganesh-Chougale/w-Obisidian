## 23.3 Technique of Interpretation

`Technique of Interpretation`: the methods or steps used to **logically explain and evaluate research findings**.

Common techniques include:

- `Compare` → compare findings with previous studies or expected results.
- `Relate` → connect findings with the research objectives or problem.
- `Explain` → give possible reasons for the findings.
- `Evaluate` → judge what the findings mean and how reliable/useful they are.

- example: A study finds that `AI-assisted students scored higher`.
  - `Compare` → previous studies also found similar results.
  - `Explain` → AI may provide faster feedback and explanations.
  - `Evaluate` → the result supports the idea that AI can assist learning, but other factors may also affect scores.

`Findings` → `Compare + Relate + Explain + Evaluate` → `Meaningful Interpretation`
