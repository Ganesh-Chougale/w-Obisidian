# 1. Preliminary Exam – Paper Format

Conducted by Maharashtra Public Service Commission
## 1A. General Studies (GS)

* **Questions:** 100 MCQs
* **Marks per question:** 2
* **Total Marks:** 200
* **Duration:** 2 hours

### Question nature:

* History (India + Maharashtra)
* Geography
* Polity
* Economy
* Environment
* Current Affairs
* Science (basic level)

### Marking:

* Correct → +2
* Wrong → **-0.5 (1/4 negative)**
* Unattempted → 0

---

## 1B. CSAT (Aptitude)

* **Questions:** 80 MCQs
* **Marks per question:** 2.5
* **Total Marks:** 200
* **Duration:** 2 hours
* **Type:** *Qualifying (need ~33% = ~66 marks)*

### Question nature:

* Reading Comprehension
* Logical Reasoning
* Analytical Ability
* Basic Maths (Class 10 level)
* Decision Making

### Marking:

* Correct → +2.5
* Wrong → **-0.625 (1/4 negative)**
* Unattempted → 0

---

## Important clarity

* Both papers are **objective (OMR-based MCQs)**
* **Language:** Marathi & English (except some comprehension parts)
* **GS marks decide cutoff**
* CSAT is just **qualifying**, but don’t ignore it

---

## Simple structure view

* Paper 1 → Knowledge-heavy
* Paper 2 → Skill/aptitude-based
