## 22.3 Variables

`Variables`: measurable characteristics or factors that are included and studied in multivariate analysis.

They can represent **different aspects of the same situation**.

- example: To study what affects exam performance:
  - `Study Time` → hours studied
  - `Attendance` → percentage of classes attended
  - `Sleep` → hours of sleep
  - `Exam Score` → marks obtained
