## 5.1 What is a Research Problem?
`Research Problem`: a specific issue, question, or gap in existing knowledge that needs to be investigated.  
→ Something we don't fully know and need to investigate.

## 5.2 Selecting the Problem
`Selecting the Problem`: choosing a research problem that is relevant, possible to study, and worth investigating.
-example: instead of researching *"How does AI affect every student in the world?"*, choosing *"How does AI-assisted study affect the exam performance of MCA students?"* because it is specific and manageable.

## 5.3 Necessity of Defining the Problem
`Defining the Problem`: clearly stating what exactly needs to be investigated, so the research stays focused and does not become too broad.
- example: instead of studying *"AI in education"*, defining the problem as *"How does AI-assisted study affect the exam performance of MCA students?"*
`Defining the Problem` → Clear Problem → Clear Direction → Focused Research
