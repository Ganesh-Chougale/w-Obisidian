# `15. Relationship between Price, EPS, and Market Cap`

These three numbers are secretly connected.

```text
EPS = Net Profit / Total Shares
```

```text
Market Cap = Share Price × Total Shares
```

```text
P/E = Share Price / EPS
```

---

# Example

Suppose a company has:

| Item         | Value      |
| ------------ | ---------- |
| Total Shares | 10 crore   |
| Net Profit   | ₹100 crore |

### Step 1 — EPS

```text
EPS = 100 / 10
EPS = ₹10
```

---

### Step 2 — Share Price

Assume market price:

```text
Share Price = ₹200
```

---

### Step 3 — Market Cap

```text
Market Cap = 200 × 10 crore
Market Cap = ₹2000 crore
```

---

### Step 4 — P/E

```text
P/E = 200 / 10
P/E = 20
```

---

# Why this connection matters

Many beginners see these numbers **individually on Screener**.

But experienced investors mentally connect them like this:

```text
Profit → EPS → Price → Market Cap
```

---

# Simple mental map

| Concept    | Meaning                    |
| ---------- | -------------------------- |
| Profit     | total money company earns  |
| EPS        | profit per share           |
| Price      | market value of each share |
| Market Cap | total company value        |
