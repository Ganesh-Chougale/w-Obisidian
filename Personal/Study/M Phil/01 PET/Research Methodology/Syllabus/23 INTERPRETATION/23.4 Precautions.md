## 23.4 Precautions

`Precautions`: steps taken during interpretation to avoid **wrong, exaggerated, or unsupported conclusions**.

- Base conclusions only on the **actual evidence**.
- Do not claim **cause and effect** when the study only shows a relationship.
- Consider the **limitations** of the research.
- Do not generalize findings beyond the studied population without sufficient evidence.
- Keep the interpretation **objective and unbiased**.

- example: If students who use AI score higher, do not conclude that **“AI definitely improves exam scores.”** The study may only show an association.

`Research Findings` → `Check Evidence + Limitations` → `Avoid Overclaiming` → `Reliable Conclusion`
